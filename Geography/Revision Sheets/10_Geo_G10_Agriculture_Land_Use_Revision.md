# G10 — Agriculture and Land Use
### Revision sheet · Geography · Phase 2 · Unit G10 · everything here is to be memorised

> **Three generative ideas.** (1) **Every crop is a point on a temperature × water chart** — overlay the climate map (G9) and the crop region follows. (2) **Physical suitability is necessary, not sufficient** — water control, holdings, credit, markets, procurement decide where farming prospers (GR: Punjab yes, Bihar no). (3) **Land is a fixed stock with competing uses** — changing the mix changes food, water and climate.
>
> **Spine:** climate × soil *so* crop range → monsoon calendar *so* kharif, rabi, zaid → irrigation, holdings, credit, markets *so* where farming thrives → GR inputs in NW *so* self-sufficiency → tropical heat, long season *so* plantations and sugar move south → surplus + roads + cities *so* agro-processing clusters → climate stress + land-use change *so* new food-security risks (satellites, CSA help).

## 2. Land use
- Nine-fold classes: forests · non-agricultural use · barren · permanent pastures · tree crops · culturable waste · current fallow · other fallow · **net sown area**.
- **NSA ~46–47%; forests ~22–23%** (2010: 47/23/30). NSA vs GCA → **cropping intensity** rises with irrigation.
- AP's NSA share < WB (true) but not because of laterite.

## 3. Crops (derive from climate)
| Crop | Needs | Regions |
|---|---|---|
| Rice | >25 °C, >100 cm / flooded, clayey alluvium | WB, UP, Punjab (irrigated), Odisha, AP–Telangana; lower Gangetic paddy + jute |
| Wheat | cool growing 10–15 °C, warm ripening, 50–75 cm + irrigation | UP, Punjab, MP, Haryana, Rajasthan |
| Millets | hot, dry, poor soil, rain-fed | Rajasthan (bajra), MH (jowar), KA (ragi); India #1 |
| Pulses | semi-arid; nitrogen fixers; black gram both seasons | MP, MH, Rajasthan; India #1 |
| Cotton | 21–30 °C, 50–100 cm, 210 frost-free days, black/light soil | Gujarat, MH, Telangana; NW & W India |
| Sugarcane | hot, humid, 75–150 cm or irrigated; thirstiest | UP (largest cane), MH, KA, TN |
| Jute | hot humid, 150–250 cm, delta alluvium | WB, Bihar, Assam (Barak) |
| Oilseeds | groundnut kharif; mustard rabi cool; soybean black soil | Gujarat, Rajasthan, MP |
- East coast more rice despite less rain (deltas).

## 4. Seasons and farming types
- **Kharif** (Jun–Jul to Sep–Oct): rice, maize, jowar, bajra, cotton, groundnut, soybean, tur, moong, jute. **Rabi** (Oct–Nov to Mar–Apr): wheat, barley, gram, mustard, peas (stored moisture, irrigation, WDs). **Zaid** (Mar–Jun): vegetables, fruits, fodder.
- Mixed farming = crops + livestock; also intensive subsistence, commercial, plantation, dry-land, shifting (jhum).

## 5. Irrigation
- ~half NSA irrigated; mostly groundwater, then canals.
- Micro-irrigation saves water & fertiliser, checks falling tables, cuts weeds & salinity. Sugarcane, paddy thirstiest; millets, pulses least.

## 6. Green Revolution *(GS-I 2014)*
- Package: HYV (Borlaug wheat; Swaminathan) + assured irrigation + fertiliser + credit + MSP/procurement (1965–68).
- **NW worked:** canals + tubewells, consolidated holdings, wheat HYVs first, cooperatives, credit, mandis, procurement.
- **East by-passed:** floods & waterlogging not controlled water · tiny fragmented holdings, zamindari, tenancy · rice HYVs later, poor fit · weak credit, power, roads, markets, procurement · low investment, out-migration. **BGREI 2010.**
- "Fertile soil + water are necessary, not sufficient."

## 7. Plantation and commercial crops *(GS-I 2014, 2022, 2013, 2017)*
- **Tea:** 20–30 °C, 150–300 cm spread, frost-free, mist, drained acid humus slopes, labour. Assam, WB (Darjeeling, Dooars), TN & Kerala (Nilgiris, Munnar), Tripura, HP (Kangra), AP. **Why not west of Darjeeling:** lower seasonal rain, long dry winter, frost, less acidic soils + distance from Kolkata port/auctions, scarce labour, cheaper Assam tea.
- **Coffee:** hot humid shade slopes — Karnataka (Chikmagalur, Kodagu), Kerala, TN. Mandya = sugar. Sri Lanka coffee lost to leaf rust.
- **Pepper:** hot humid slopes <~1,100 m, ~200 cm. Cardamom: Kerala.
- **Rubber (2022):** equatorial >200 cm. **Thailand** (largest), Indonesia, Vietnam, **India** (Kerala, Tripura/NE), **Côte d'Ivoire** (rising), Malaysia, China (Hainan, Yunnan). Issues: forest clearing, biodiversity, monoculture erosion & slope landslides, agrochemicals, water, carbon, land conflicts; certification, EU deforestation regulation (delayed).
- **Sugar south (2013):** higher yield & sucrose; crushing ~6–7 months vs ~4 north; cooperatives (MH); newer bigger mills; ports. Caveats: UP largest cane; drought (Marathwada); ethanol diversion — **E20 reached 2025**.
- **Pulses (2017):** fix nitrogen (Rhizobium) · little water, drought-tolerant · cheap protein · intercropping & rotation, soil health · low carbon/water footprint. India #1 producer (~25.7 Mt 2024-25), consumer, importer; gram biggest; **Mission for Aatmanirbharta in Pulses (Budget 2025-26)**.
- **World:** cocoa Côte d'Ivoire & Ghana · New World tobacco, cocoa, rubber · rice least traded share.

## 8. Fisheries *(GS-I 2018)*
- India #2 fish producer (~18 Mt; ~¾ inland, aquaculture — AP shrimp & carp, WB, Odisha).
- Problems: overfishing, juvenile catch, pollution/dead zones, seed & disease, cold chain & harbours, credit/insurance, climate shifts.
- Strategies: **PMMSY 2020**, FIDF, KCC for fishers, reservoir cage culture, seaweed, deep-sea vessels, harbours, fishing bans, certification.

## 9. Agro-processing and non-farm primary *(GS-I 2019, 2025)*
- **NW agro-processing localisation:** surplus wheat, rice, milk, cane, potato, kinnow · roads, power, cold chains, mandis · NCR market, FCI · mega food parks, PM Kisan Sampada · cooperatives (dairy) · limits: monoculture, water, stubble burning, small farmer value share.
- **Non-farm primary (fishing, forestry, mining, quarrying, animal husbandry, gathering):** coasts → fishing, salt, sand · Himalaya/NE → forestry, bamboo, medicinal plants, transhumance (Gaddis, Bakarwals, Bhotias) · plateau → mining (Chota Nagpur, Odisha, KA), tendu, mahua · Thar → camel/sheep/goat (Raika), Sambhar salt · plains → dairy (Operation Flood), poultry, inland fish · Deccan → Deccani sheep, sericulture (KA mulberry; Manipur oak tasar).

## 10. Food security *(GS-I 2023 ×2)*
- **2024-25 (final, 20 Nov 2025):** foodgrains **357.73 Mt** (rice 150.18, wheat 117.94, coarse 63.92, pulses 25.68) · horticulture **362.08 Mt** · agri exports **~$51.1 bn** · rice exports $12.95 bn · millets 18.59 Mt · #1 rice exporter; #2 rice & wheat producer.
- **Importer → exporter:** HYV + irrigation · MSP & FCI · roads, power, credit · ICAR/CGIAR · White, Blue, Yellow revolutions · horticulture · export policy. Caveat: **virtual water** from depleting aquifers; nutrition gaps.
- Hook: 1966 PL-480 "ship-to-mouth"; Shastri's skip-a-meal appeal.
- **Climate & tropical food security:** heat at crop limits (March 2022 wheat) · erratic monsoon (2026) · floods, cyclones, delta salinity · pests · CO₂ lowers protein, zinc, iron · fish shift · price shocks. Adapt: heat-tolerant varieties, insurance, millets, harvesting, early warning.

## 11. Satellites and land-use change *(GS-I 2026 ×2, 2021)*
- **Pipeline:** Resourcesat, Cartosat (optical) · RISAT, EOS-04 (radar) · **NISAR (NASA–ISRO, Jul 2025)** → FASAL (crop forecast), NADAMS (drought), soil moisture, CHAMAN (horticulture), flood/pest/fire alerts → **Krishi-DSS (2024)**, **YES-TECH (2023) for PMFBY**, **Digital Crop Survey (AgriStack)**, residue-burning tracking → productivity, adaptation, mitigation, stable supply, fast compensation. Limits: small plots, cloud, ground truth, farmer access, privacy.
- **Land-use change drivers:** urbanisation, infrastructure, industry & mining, irrigation/cropping shifts, forest diversion, plantations, climate stress.
- **Consequences:** farmland lost near cities · **water bodies reclaimed → urban floods, lost recharge, heat islands, biodiversity loss (2021)** · fragmentation · degradation (G2) · altered local climate (G9) · displacement, conflict. Responses: zoning, wetland rules, DILRMP, green belts, floodplain zoning.
