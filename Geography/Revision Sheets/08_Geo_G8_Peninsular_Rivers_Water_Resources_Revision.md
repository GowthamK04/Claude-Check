# G8 — Peninsular Rivers and Water Resources
### Revision sheet · Geography · Phase 2 · Unit G8 · everything here is to be memorised

> **Three generative ideas.** (1) **Peninsula tilts east, rocks are hard:** big rivers east to deltas; rift rivers (Narmada, Tapi) and Ghats streams west to estuaries; little groundwater storage. (2) **Rain concentrated in time and space:** floods then droughts; regional stress; need for storage. (3) **Groundwater = overdrawn bank:** cheap power + thirsty crops → falling tables, seawater intrusion, drier rivers; fixes slow the water and put it back.
>
> **Spine:** east tilt *so* deltas east → rifts + scarp *so* estuaries west → ~75% rain in 4 months *so* floods then droughts → little storage + hard rock *so* only ~¼ usable → cheap power + thirsty crops *so* aquifers overdrawn → overdraft *so* tables fall, salt water intrudes, summer rivers dry → slow · sink · store · share *so* water security.

## 2. Peninsular drainage *(generative)*
- **East tilt** → Mahanadi, Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri, Pennar → Bay, deltas.
- **Rift valleys** → Narmada (Vindhya–Satpura), Tapi (Satpura–Ajanta) flow west. **WG scarp** → short swift west streams.
- Kerala has east-flowing (Kabini, Bhavani, Pambar); MP has west-flowing (Narmada, Tapi). Kalinadi flows **west**.
- Patterns: radial (Amarkantak), trellis (Vindhyan), rectangular, dendritic.
- Seasonal rain-fed; **Kaveri steadier** — both SW (Karnataka) and NE (TN) monsoons.

## 3. East-flowing systems *(leaf)*
- **Godavari** — Trimbakeshwar (Nashik); longest (~1,465 km); "Dakshin Ganga"; Pravara, Manjira (Nizam Sagar), Penganga, **Pranhita = Wardha + Wainganga**, Indravati (Jagdalpur), Sabari; Polavaram.
- **Krishna** — Mahabaleshwar; Koyna, Ghataprabha, Malaprabha, Bhima (Chandrabhaga, Pandharpur), Tungabhadra (Hampi), Musi (Hyderabad); Almatti, Srisailam, Nagarjuna Sagar.
- **Kaveri** — Talakaveri (Brahmagiri, Kodagu); Hemavati, Kabini, Bhavani, Amaravati; forks thrice: **Srirangapatna, Shivanasamudra, Srirangam**; Mettur.
- **Mahanadi** — Sihawa (Chhattisgarh); Hirakud; Odisha delta.
- **Pennar** — **Gandikota canyon**. Vaigai, Tamraparni (TN). Subarnarekha, Brahmani, Baitarani (Chota Nagpur). **Nagavali, Vamsadhara** — Eastern Ghats.
- N→S: Subarnarekha–Mahanadi–Godavari–Krishna–Pennar–Kaveri–Vaigai. Length: Godavari > Narmada > Mahanadi > Tapi.
- **Damodar** — down-warped rift; coal; DVC.

## 4. West-flowing; delta vs estuary *(GS-I 2013)*
- Delta = fan of distributaries on shallow shelf (east). Estuary = drowned funnel mouth, tides (west).
- **Why no deltas:** (1) short, steep, fast → little time to deposit, small catchments; (2) Narmada/Tapi in hard-rock rifts → less loose load, faulted mouths; (3) narrow steep shelf, submergent coast → silt lost to deep water; (4) strong tides & monsoon waves sweep sediment (Gulf of Khambhat). They **do** carry silt.
- **Narmada** — Amarkantak; marble rocks & **Dhuandhar** (Bhedaghat, Jabalpur), **Kapildhara**; Sardar Sarovar (Gujarat), Indira Sagar (MP). **Tapi** — Betul; Ukai; Surat. Mahi, Sabarmati; **Luni → Rann**; Sharavati (Jog), Netravati, Kalinadi, Mandovi, Zuari, Periyar, Bharathapuzha.

## 5. Falls, wetlands, reservoirs *(leaf)*
- **Falls:** Jog/Gersoppa (Sharavati) · Shivanasamudram (Kaveri) · Dhuandhar, Kapildhara (Narmada) · Hundru (Subarnarekha).
- **Wetlands:** Hokera (J&K) · Renuka (HP) · Rudrasagar (Tripura) · Sasthamkotta (Kerala) · Kodaikanal (artificial).
- **Reservoirs:** Aliyar (TN), Isapur (MH), Kangsabati (WB) · Ghataprabha (KA) · Gandhi Sagar, Indira Sagar (MP) · Maithon (JH) · Ukai (Tapi) · Govind Sagar (Sutlej).
- **Dams:** Mettur–Kaveri · Almatti–Krishna · Sardar Sarovar–Narmada · Gandhi Sagar–Chambal · Nizam Sagar–Manjira.
- **Towns:** Betul–Tapi · Jagdalpur–Indravati · Jabalpur–Narmada · Ujjain–Shipra · Pandharpur–Chandrabhaga · Tiruchirappalli–Kaveri.

## 6. Projects, floods *(GS-I 2017)*
- **DVC 1948** — first multipurpose of independent India (TVA model); now thermal + gas. Bhakra-Nangal, Hirakud, Nagarjuna Sagar, Sardar Sarovar, Polavaram. "Temples of modern India."
- Floods rising: siltation, floodplain/wetland encroachment, deforestation, failing embankments, extreme rain, paving.
- **Floods → irrigation & navigation:** store peaks (reservoirs, tanks) for rabi · recharge aquifers (MAR, **Bhungroo**, paleochannels) · divert surplus (Pattiseema, links) · regulated flows for NWs · limits: silt, displacement, ecology. Big dams can worsen floods if releases mismanaged (Kerala 2018).

## 7. Inland waterways *(GS-I 2016)*
- **NW Act 2016 → 111 NWs** (106 new + 5); IWAI. **NW-1** Ganga Haldia–Prayagraj (~1,620 km; Jal Marg Vikas, World Bank; terminals Varanasi, Sahibganj, Haldia) · **NW-2** Brahmaputra Sadiya–Dhubri (~891 km) · **NW-3** West Coast Canal, Kerala (Kollam–Kottapuram).
- Pros: cheapest per tonne-km, low emissions, decongestion, bulk cargo, Indo-Bangladesh Protocol route. Cons: low dry-season depth, silt, shifting channels, low bridges, few terminals, dolphins vs dredging. Way forward: river training, night navigation, Ro-Ro/Ro-Pax, Sagarmala.

## 8. Water budget & stress *(GS-I 2015, 2019, 2023)*
- **~4,000 BCM** rain → **~1,999 BCM** available → **~1,122 BCM usable** (690 surface + 432 ground) (CWC).
- **18% people, 4% water.** Per capita **~1,486 m³ (2021) → ~1,367 (2031)**. Stress <1,700; scarcity <1,000. Agriculture ~80% of withdrawals. CWMI 2018: ~600 mn high–extreme stress.
- **Five causes:** timing (~75% rain Jun–Sep) · geography (Meghalaya >1,000 cm vs W Rajasthan <15 cm; Brahmaputra surplus, Pennar deficit) · storage & hard rock · use (flood-irrigated paddy, sugarcane; leaks; little reuse) · quality & governance (arsenic, fluoride, nitrate, sewage; State subject; Kaveri, Krishna, Mahadayi disputes; climate — 2026 monsoon 87%).
- **2019 regions:** NW overdraft · Deccan hard rock (Marathwada, Telangana) · arid west · cities (Chennai 2019, Bengaluru 2024) · saline coasts · Himalayan springs · NE floods.
- Hook: **Chennai 2019** — ~1,400 mm rain, water by train from Jolarpettai.

## 9. Groundwater *(GS-I 2024; GS-III 2025)*
- **CGWB 2025:** recharge **448.52 BCM** · extractable **407.75** · extraction **247.22** → **SoE 60.63%** · safe 73.14% · semi-critical 11.21% · critical 2.97% · **over-exploited 10.8%** (730 of 6,762 units) · saline 1.88% · **87% to agriculture** · safe share 62.6% (2017) → 73.1% (2025). Categories: safe <70%, semi 70–90, critical 90–100, OE >100.
- OE clusters: NW (Punjab, Haryana, Delhi, W UP) · W (Rajasthan, Gujarat) · S (KA, TN, Telangana, AP).
- **Factors:** free/flat power · MSP rice–wheat · paddy in Punjab–Haryana, sugarcane in MH · flood irrigation · unmetered tubewells, ownership tied to land · paving, lost lakes · erratic monsoon · hard-rock aquifers.
- **Gangetic valley (2024):** NW granary overdraft + arsenic in middle/lower plain · **IIT Roorkee 2025: groundwater sustains much of the Ganga's summer flow** · rising costs, failing shallow wells, rice–wheat & procurement risk, health.
- **Steps:** **Atal Bhujal Yojana 2020** (7 states, community) · NAQUIM · CGWA permits · Jal Shakti Abhiyan "Catch the Rain" · **Mission Amrit Sarovar 2022** · Per Drop More Crop (PMKSY) · PM-KUSUM (needs caps) · **Punjab Preservation of Subsoil Water Act 2009** · crop diversification · JJM source sustainability.
- Wrong model: "running out everywhere" — crisis is local and severe.

## 10. Seawater intrusion *(GS-III 2025)*
- Fresh floats on denser seawater; **Ghyben–Herzberg: 1 m fall in table → ~40 m rise of salt interface**.
- Causes: over-pumping · less river flow/recharge (upstream dams) · SLR & surges · sand mining, aquaculture · lost mangroves, dunes.
- Where: Saurashtra–Kachchh · **Minjur (Chennai)** · coastal AP, Odisha, Kerala · Sundarbans.
- Remedies: regulate coastal pumping · artificial recharge (check dams, percolation ponds, injection wells) · tidal regulators, subsurface barriers (**bandharas**) · conjunctive use, desalination (Chennai) · mangroves, dunes · salinity monitoring.

## 11. Harvesting, watersheds, efficiency *(GS-I 2016, 2018)*
- **Micro-watershed:** ridge (trees, contour trenches) → slope (bunds, terraces, gully plugs) → drainage line (check dams, gabions, nala bunds) → valley (farm ponds, percolation tanks, recharge wells). **Ralegan Siddhi, Hiware Bazar**; PMKSY WDC 2.0.
- **Urban RWH:** rooftop + recharge pits (**Chennai mandatory 2003**) · lake restoration (Bengaluru) · permeable paving, sponge cities · treated wastewater reuse · fix leaks (NRW 30–40%).
- **Traditional:** johad, kund (Rajasthan) · baoli · eri (TN) · kuhl (HP) · zabo (Nagaland) · bamboo drip (Meghalaya).
- Efficiency: drip/sprinkler, direct-seeded rice, millets & pulses in dry zones, industrial pricing, JJM.

## 12. Interlinking *(GS-I 2020)*
- **NPP 1980**, NWDA; **30 links (14 Himalayan, 16 Peninsular)**. **Ken–Betwa** first (foundation Dec 2024; Panna Tiger Reserve). **Godavari–Krishna: Pattiseema** (2016); Polavaram.
- For: drought irrigation, floods, power, navigation, drinking water. Against: cost, submergence/displacement, forests & wildlife, shrinking "surplus", deltas starved of silt/fresh water, inter-state consent, seismic/engineering, transboundary.
- **Reforms (mechanism · institution · accountability):** metered/capped farm power + cash support · states, DISCOMs, SERCs · subsidy audits | crop-switch procurement · Agriculture Ministry, FCI · block water-saving targets | community aquifer budgets · gram panchayats + CGWB · funds on measured outcomes | basin authorities with e-flows · Jal Shakti, basin bodies · annual basin water account to legislatures.

## 13. Transboundary *(GS-I 2016, 2026)*
- **IWT 1960 (World Bank):** eastern Ravi, Beas, Sutlej → India; western Indus, Jhelum, Chenab → Pakistan (limited Indian run-of-river use; Baglihar within parameters); survived 3 wars; **in abeyance April 2025** (Pahalgam).
- **Ganga Water Treaty 12 Dec 1996** (Deve Gowda–Hasina; Farakka lean-season sharing) — **expires 12 Dec 2026**; talks on; Bangladesh wants guarantee clause; Padma barrage (target 2033); no Teesta deal; 54 shared rivers; JRC 1972.
- **Brahmaputra:** no treaty; flood-data MoUs; **China's Medog (lower Yarlung Tsangpo) dam began July 2025**; India's Upper Siang buffer.
- **Nepal:** Kosi 1954, Gandak 1959, Mahakali 1996 (Pancheshwar). **Bhutan:** Chukha, Tala, Mangdechhu hydro sold to India.
- **2026:** asset (power trade, navigation, data, irrigation) vs conflict (distrust, dams, climate, politics) → basin data-sharing, benefit-sharing, climate clauses, insulate water from security.
