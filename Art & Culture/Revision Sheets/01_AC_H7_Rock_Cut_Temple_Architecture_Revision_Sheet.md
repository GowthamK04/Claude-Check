## 1. Rock art (Mains 2015)

- **Hook:** Wakankar saw Bhimbetka's rocks from a train (1957). Carlleyle found India's first rock painting in 1867–68, 12 years before Altamira.
- **Bhimbetka:** Raisen district, MP, Vindhyas; UNESCO 2003; 400+ painted shelters in five clusters.
- **Other sites:** Lakhudiyar (Almora, Suyal river; figures holding hands, wavy lines), Mirzapur–Kaimur, Kupgallu, Piklihal and Tekkalkota (Karnataka), Edakkal petroglyphs (Wayanad, Kerala).
- **Three phases:**
  - **Upper Palaeolithic:** green and dark red outlines; huge bison, elephant, tiger, rhino, boar; stick men.
  - **Mesolithic (richest):** most paintings, smaller figures; group hunts with barbed spears, bows and arrows; dancing, honey collection, children, women grinding; naturalistic and "X-ray" animals; red and white most common.
  - **Chalcolithic:** pottery and metal; exchange with Malwa farmers.
- **Pigments:** red from haematite (geru), green from chalcedony, white probably limestone; binder of animal fat, gum or resin. Survival came from oxide reaction with the rock and shelter from rain.
- **2015 "critically":** comparable in technique and vitality, different in purpose (ritual, magic, story); dating uncertain; UPSC's "rock-cut architecture" means rock art.

## 2. Rock-cut caves (Mains 2020)

- **Why carve:** monsoon retreat (varshavasa) · merit for donors · trade routes from Konkan ports · carvable Deccan basalt.
- **Chaitya = pray:** apsidal hall, stupa at the apse, nave and aisles for pradakshina, barrel vault, horseshoe *gavaksha* window. Karle (largest), Bhaja, Ajanta 9/10/19/26, Ellora 10.
- **Vihara = live:** square hall, cells on three sides, verandah; later a Buddha shrine at the back. Nasik, Ajanta 1/16/17, Ellora 12 (Tin Thal, three storeys).
- **Evolution and its ideas:** Mauryan (timber copied in stone) → Western Deccan (chaitya + vihara on trade routes) → Gupta–Vakataka (the image enters) → Chalukya–Pallava (Hindu shrines; the monolith as model temple) → Rashtrakuta Kailasa (carving peaks, then building takes over).

## 3. Temple grammar and the three styles

- **Grammar:** garbhagriha (womb-house) · mandapa (hall) · antarala (vestibule) · shikhara/vimana (tower) · amalaka + kalasha (Nagara top) · pradakshina patha · jagati (platform) · prakara + gopuram (wall + gateway) · vahana on the axis · **panchayatana** = main shrine + four corner shrines (2014).
- **Trap:** in the south "shikhara" means only the dome cap; the whole tower is the vimana.
- **Birth (Gupta):** Sanchi Temple 17 (flat roof, porch) → Deogarh Dashavatara (c. 500, panchayatana, early shikhara, Vishnu on Ananta) · Bhitargaon (brick).

| Feature | Nagara | Dravida | Vesara |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tower | Curvilinear shikhara, amalaka, urushringas | Stepped vimana of talas with kuta/shala miniatures; dome cap | Stepped tiers on a curving profile |
| Plan | Square with projections (ratha) | Square in a walled court | Often stellate |
| Platform | High jagati | Ground level | High jagati |
| Enclosure | Usually none | Prakara, gopurams, tank | Usually none |
| Door guards | Ganga and Yamuna | Dvarapalas | Mixed |
| Material | Sandstone, marble | Granite, sandstone | Soapstone (Hoysala) |
| Sub-forms | Latina, phamsana, valabhi | Square, rectangular, gajaprishtha, circular, octagonal vimanas | Chalukya → Hoysala, Kakatiya |

- **Odisha:** rekha deul → jagamohana → nata mandira → bhoga mandapa; plain interiors, walled compounds. Parasurameswara (7th) → Mukteshvara (10th, torana) → Lingaraja (11th) → Jagannath, Puri (12th, Anantavarman Chodaganga) → **Konark** (13th, Narasimhadeva I, Eastern Ganga; Sun's chariot, 24 wheels, 7 horses; UNESCO 1984).
- **Khajuraho (Chandela):** about 25 of 85 survive, Hindu and Jain, c. 950–1050; UNESCO 1986; high platform, cascading urushringas. Lakshmana (954, panchayatana), Kandariya Mahadeva (c. 1030, Vidyadhara).
- **Solanki:** Modhera Sun Temple (1026–27, Bhima I; Surya Kund) · Dilwara Jain temples, Mount Abu (Vimal Vasahi 1031, Luna Vasahi 1230) · Rani ki Vav (UNESCO 2014).
- **Kashmir and hills:** Martand Sun (8th c., Lalitaditya; trefoil arches) · Masrur · Jageshwar · Katarmal (Sun).
- **Sun temples:** Konark, Modhera, Martand, Arasavalli (Srikakulam, AP), Katarmal. Amarkantak = Narmada source; Omkareshwar = Jyotirlinga (2017).
- **Yogini temples:** circular, roofless, 64 cells, Tantric Shakta. Mitaoli (Morena; Kachchhapaghata Devapala); Hirapur and Ranipur-Jharial (Odisha); Bhedaghat. The Parliament link is a popular belief (2021).
- **Somnath:** Jyotirlinga; described by Al-Biruni; Mahmud's raid 1025; rebuilt and consecrated by **President Rajendra Prasad** in 1951 (2022).
- **Dravida:** Pallava in three steps: rock mandapas (Mahendravarman I) → monolithic rathas (Narasimhavarman I Mamalla) → built Shore Temple and Kailasanatha, Kanchi (Rajasimha). Chola: Brihadisvara, Thanjavur (Rajaraja I, 1010; 66 m granite vimana), Gangaikondacholapuram (Rajendra I), Airavatesvara, Darasuram (Rajaraja II); UNESCO 1987, extended 2004. Pandya onward the gopuram outgrows the vimana; Vijayanagara and Nayaka add raya gopurams, yali pillars, kalyana mandapas (Hampi, Meenakshi, Srirangam).
- **Deccan laboratory:** Aihole ("cradle"; apsidal Durga temple; Jain Meguti 634 with Ravikirti's inscription) · Pattadakal (UNESCO 1987): **Papanatha = Nagara, Virupaksha = Dravida** (c. 740s, Queen Lokamahadevi, victory over the Pallavas) · Badami's Malegitti = Dravida · Aihole's Huchimalligudi = Nagara (2026).
- **Hoysala:** Belur Chennakeshava (1117, Vishnuvardhana), Halebidu Hoysaleswara, Somanathapura Keshava (1268); star plan, soapstone, lathe-turned pillars, friezes; UNESCO 2023 (India's 42nd).
- **Kakatiya Ramappa:** Palampet, Telangana, 1213; floating bricks, dancer brackets; UNESCO 2021.

## 4. Sculpture and motifs (Mains 2022 ×2, 2025)

- **Mudras:** abhaya = fear not · varada = boon · dhyana = meditation · **bhumisparsha = Earth called to witness his purity against Mara** (2012) · dharmachakra = first sermon · vitarka = discussion.
- **Aniconic symbols (NCERT XII):** **empty seat = meditation** (2026) · stupa = mahaparinibbana · wheel = first sermon · Bodhi tree = enlightenment · riderless horse = the Great Departure.
- **Tribhanga:** one leg bent, body curved oppositely at waist and neck (2013). **Kayotsarga:** Jain standing meditation.
- **Chandella (2025):** *vigour* = tribhanga, deep relief, motion; *breadth* = gods, surasundaris (kohl, letter, thorn, mirror), musicians, teachers, armies, hunts, masons, mithuna (a small share); *why* = purusharthas, skilled guilds, confident patrons (Vidyadhara resisted Mahmud).
- **Social life (2022):** dress → Belur madanikas · dance → Chidambaram 108 karanas, Ramappa → Perini revival · war → Hoysala friezes · trade → horses and foreign traders at Hazara Rama, Hampi · festival → Mahanavami · work and family → Khajuraho, Konark. **Limit:** idealised and court-centred; a window, not a photograph.
- **Lion lane:** absent on Harappan seals → Sarnath, Sanchi, Vaishali capitals (lion's roar of dhamma) → Shakyasimha, simhasana, Mahavira's lanchhana → Durga, Narasimha, kirtimukha, Pallava lion pillars, yalis, Hoysala emblem → State Emblem, 26 Jan 1950 (Satyameva Jayate, Mundaka Upanishad).
- **Bull lane:** Harappan zebu seals, Pashupati seal → Indra as vrishabha, Rampurva bull capital (Rashtrapati Bhavan) → Rishabhanatha's lanchhana, Vima Kadphises' Shiva with bull → Nandi facing every Shiva sanctum (Thanjavur, Lepakshi), dharma as a bull → Mattu Pongal, Pola; bull on the emblem's abacus.

## 5. Philosophy into stone (Mains 2020, with H9)

- **Why the dark sanctum:** it is a womb; the journey runs from the crowded outside to one bare centre.
- **Stupa as cosmos:** anda (egg; relics at its heart) · harmika (abode of gods) · yashti (world axis) · chhatra (honour, heavens) · medhi and pradakshina (walk clockwise like the sun) · vedika and torana.
- **Idea → form:** early Buddhism → aniconic symbols · Mahayana → Buddha images, bodhisattvas (Ajanta) · Jainism → naked standing tirthankara (Gommateshwara 981), temple-cities (Dilwara, Palitana) · cosmology → vastu-purusha mandala (8 × 8), shikhara as Meru/Kailasa, Konark as the Sun's chariot · purusharthas → Khajuraho · Shaiva Siddhanta → Nataraja · Bhakti → halls, chariots, gopurams · Tantra → yogini circles.
- **Counter-view:** royal legitimacy (Thanjavur, Pattadakal) · temples as banks and landlords · guilds working across sects.

## 6. Counter-cases to drop into answers

- Deccan ≠ automatically Vesara (Virupaksha, Malegitti are Dravida).
- Khajuraho is not "erotic art"; erotic panels are a small fraction.
- Caves are not only Buddhist (Ajivika, Jain, Hindu, all three at Ellora).
- The stupa pre-dates Buddhism (2023, H3).
- Early Dravida is vimana-centred; gopurams dominate only after the 12th–13th century.
- Empty seat: UPSC says meditation; other books say enlightenment.
