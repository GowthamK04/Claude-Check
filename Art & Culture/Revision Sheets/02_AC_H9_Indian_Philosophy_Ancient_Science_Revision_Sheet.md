## 1. The dividing line

- **Astika** = accepts the authority of the Vedas; **nastika** = rejects it. It is *not* about God: Samkhya and Mimamsa have no creator yet are astika.
- Astika: Nyaya, Vaisheshika, Samkhya, Yoga, Mimamsa, Vedanta. Nastika: Charvaka, Ajivika, Buddhism, Jainism.
- 2014 trap: "Lokayata and Kapalika" is not a pair of the six systems (Lokayata = Charvaka; Kapalika = Shaiva ascetic sect).

## 2. The six darshanas

| School | Founder · text | Core idea | Liberation by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nyaya | Gautama (Akshapada) · Nyaya Sutra | Logic; 4 pramanas: perception, inference, comparison, testimony; later Navya-Nyaya (Gangesha, Mithila) | Right knowledge |
| Vaisheshika | Kanada · Vaisheshika Sutra | Atoms (paramanu); categories (padarthas) | Knowledge of categories |
| Samkhya | Kapila · Samkhya Karika (Ishvarakrishna) | Purusha (consciousness) + prakriti (matter, 3 gunas: sattva, rajas, tamas); originally no creator; **accepts rebirth** | **Self-knowledge**: telling spirit from matter (2013) |
| Yoga | Patanjali · Yoga Sutra | Eight limbs: yama, niyama, asana, pranayama, pratyahara, dharana, dhyana, samadhi; accepts Ishvara | Stilling the mind |
| Mimamsa (Purva) | Jaimini · Mimamsa Sutra | Vedas eternal, self-valid; ritual duty; shaped legal reasoning; Kumarila Bhatta, Prabhakara | **Karma**: right ritual action (1995) |
| Vedanta (Uttara) | Badarayana · Brahma Sutra | Atman–Brahman; prasthanatrayi = Upanishads + Brahma Sutra + Gita | Knowledge or devotion, by branch |

- **Pairs:** Nyaya (how we know) + Vaisheshika (what exists) · Samkhya (theory) + Yoga (practice) · Mimamsa (ritual) + Vedanta (knowledge).
- **Pegs:** "Good Kids Keep Practicing Just Briefly" = Gautama, Kanada, Kapila, Patanjali, Jaimini, Badarayana · "Nyaya knows, Vaisheshika counts; Samkhya explains, Yoga trains; Mimamsa acts, Vedanta realises."

## 3. Vedanta branches

| School | Teacher | Soul–God–world | Path |
|---|---|---|---|
| Advaita (non-dualism) | Shankara, 8th c. | Only Brahman real; soul = Brahman; world = maya | Jnana; mathas at Sringeri, Dwarka, Puri, Jyotirmath |
| Vishishtadvaita (qualified) | Ramanuja, 11th–12th c., Srirangam | Souls and world real, as God's "body" | **Devotion and surrender (bhakti, prapatti)** (2022); Statue of Equality, Hyderabad |
| Dvaita (dualism) | Madhva, 13th c., Udupi | God, souls, matter eternally distinct | Bhakti |
| Dvaitadvaita | Nimbarka | Difference and non-difference | Radha–Krishna |
| Shuddhadvaita (pure) | Vallabha, c. 1500 | World is Krishna's own form | **Pushti Marg** (2014 trap: not Madhva) |
| Achintya-bhedabheda | Chaitanya, 16th c. | Inconceivable oneness and difference | Kirtan (H11) |

- **Nathamuni** (10th c.) = Sri Vaishnava teacher, compiled the Alvar hymns (2022).
- **Arc to write:** ritual (Mimamsa) → knowledge (Advaita) → devotion (Ramanuja → Bhakti).

## 4. Charvaka and Ajivika

- **Charvaka / Lokayata** (Brihaspati, by tradition): only perception is valid; no soul, heaven, rebirth or karma; consciousness arises from matter like intoxication from fermented grain; priests and rituals are frauds. Known mainly through critics.
- **Ajivika** (Makkhali Gosala, once a companion of Mahavira): **niyati**, fate fixes everything; effort cannot hasten liberation; Barabar caves gifted by Ashoka and Dasharatha (H7).
- **Why rejecting the Vedas mattered:** it rejected the priests' monopoly; it spoke to merchants and artisans; Prakrit and Pali were used instead of only Sanskrit.

## 5. Buddhist philosophy and Mahayana

- **Core:** anicca (impermanence), dukkha (unsatisfactoriness), **anatta** (no permanent self), pratitya-samutpada (dependent origination). **Nirvana = extinction of the flame of desire**, not annihilation of a self (2013).

| | Theravada | Mahayana |
|---|---|---|
| Buddha | Human teacher | Deified, eternal (2019) |
| Ideal | Arhat | **Bodhisattva**, who delays own nirvana to help all (2016) |
| Path | Eightfold Path | **Paramitas** = perfections: generosity, morality, patience, effort, meditation, wisdom (2020) |
| Worship | Little ritual; aniconic art | Images, ritual, devotion (2019) |
| Canon · region | Pali · Sri Lanka, SE Asia | Sanskrit · China, Japan, Korea, Tibet |

- **Bodhisattvas:** Avalokiteshvara = **Padmapani** = Lokeshvara (compassion; Ajanta Cave 1) · Manjushri (wisdom) · Vajrapani (power) · **Maitreya = future Buddha** (2018).
- **Epithets:** Shakyamuni, Tathagata ("thus-gone"); **Nayaputta = Mahavira** (2024).
- **Schools:** Madhyamaka: **Nagarjuna** (2nd c., Andhra), **shunyata**; his disciple **Aryadeva** was Buddhist, not Jain (2022) · Yogachara: Asanga and Vasubandhu (4th c.), "mind-only" · Logic: **Dignaga** (2022), Dharmakirti · Vajrayana: Tantric, Palas, Tibet.
- **Texts:** Nettipakarana (Pali) and Avadanashataka (Sanskrit) are Buddhist (2022).

## 6. Jain philosophy

- **No creator;** the universe is eternal and runs by **universal law** (2011).
- **Jiva** (soul) and **ajiva** (matter, space, time, motion, rest). Every particle may hold a soul: earth, water, fire, air bodies (2013, 2023). Hook: the monk's brush and mouth-cloth.
- **Karma = fine matter** clinging to the soul; its bane; destroyed by **penance** and right conduct (2013).
- **Four gatis:** deva, manushya, tiryancha (animals and plants), **naraka** (hell-beings); **yaksha is not one** (2026).
- **Triratna:** right faith, right knowledge, right conduct. Five vows: ahimsa, satya, asteya, aparigraha, brahmacharya; laity keep **anuvratas** (1995).
- **Anekantavada:** reality is many-sided; **syadvada:** every statement is true "in some respect"; blind men and the elephant → pluralism, tolerance.
- **Sects:** Digambara (sky-clad; women not liberated in this life) · Shvetambara (white-clad) → **Sthanakvasi** (2018) and Terapanthi, both non-image-worshipping.
- **Texts:** Agamas (Acharanga Sutra) · **Parishishtaparvan** (Hemachandra, 12th c., Gujarat) · **Trishashtilakshana Mahapurana** (63 great figures) (2022).

## 7. Ancient science

- **Hook:** Bakhshali manuscript (found 1881 near Peshawar): a dot for zero; Bodleian radiocarbon dating (2017) put its oldest leaves in the 3rd–4th c.; the dating is debated.

| Person / text | Period | Remember |
|---|---|---|
| Baudhayana, Sulba Sutras | c. 800–500 BCE | Altar geometry; "Pythagoras" theorem; √2 approximation |
| Panini, Ashtadhyayi | c. 4th c. BCE | ~4,000 rules generating Sanskrit; a model formal system |
| Charaka Samhita | 1st–2nd c. CE (tradition: Kushana court) | Internal medicine; tridosha (vata, pitta, kapha) |
| Sushruta Samhita | layers BCE–CE | Surgery, rhinoplasty, cataract, 100+ instruments; **not organ transplant** |
| Mehrauli iron pillar | c. 400, Chandragupta II | Rust-free: phosphorus-rich iron, protective film |
| Aryabhata, Aryabhatiya | 499 | π ≈ 3.1416; sine (jya); Earth rotates; eclipses = shadows |
| Varahamihira | 6th c. | Pancha Siddhantika; Brihat Samhita (encyclopaedia) |
| Brahmagupta, Brahmasphutasiddhanta | 628 | Rules for zero and negatives; cyclic quadrilateral |
| Vagbhata, Ashtanga Hridaya | 7th c. | Third Ayurvedic classic (Brihat-trayi with Charaka, Sushruta) |
| Bhaskara II | 1150 | Lilavati, Bijaganita, Siddhanta Shiromani |
| Madhava of Sangamagrama | 14th c. | Kerala school; infinite series for π, sine, cosine |

- **Also:** Nagarjuna the alchemist (rasayana, mercury) · wootz crucible steel · zinc distillation at Zawar (Rajasthan) · Lagadha's Vedanga Jyotisha · Surya Siddhanta · numerals carried west by al-Khwarizmi (9th c.) · jya → jiba → "sinus".
- **Why it slowed:** theory split from craft (caste), decline of Nalanda and Vikramashila, commentary over experiment; the Kerala school kept innovating.
- **Say it accurately:** India gave zero *as a number with rules* and the place-value system; Babylon and the Maya had placeholders.

## 8. Counter-cases

- Astika ≠ theist.
- "Indian thought is only spiritual": Charvaka, Nyaya, Vaisheshika and Buddhist logic show a rational strand (Sen's "argumentative Indian").
- Pushti Marg is Vallabha's; the bodhisattva ideal is Mahayana; Sushruta did plastic surgery, not organ transplants.
