## 2. Sources

- **Arthashastra** — Kautilya (Chanakya / Vishnugupta); 15 books; saptanga, mandala, spies, adhyakshas. Rediscovered **1905** by **R. Shamasastry** (Mysore), published 1909. Date debated.
- **Indica** — **Megasthenes**, ambassador of **Seleucus**; lost, survives in quotes (Strabo, Arrian, Diodorus). First foreign visitor in the 1999 order (Megasthenes → Fa-hien → Hiuen Tsang → I-tsing).
- **Mudrarakshasa** — **Vishakhadatta**, Gupta-period play; Chanakya vs Rakshasa; Chandragupta's court intrigue (2002).
- **Buddhist/Jain:** Dipavamsa, Mahavamsa, Divyavadana, Ashokavadana; Hemachandra's Parishishtaparvan.
- **Inscriptions:** edicts; Rudradaman's **Junagadh** (150 CE) names Chandragupta's governor **Pushyagupta**; **Sohgaura** (UP) and **Mahasthan** (Bangladesh) plates on grain storage (1998: Sohgaura earliest).
- **Archaeology:** Kumrahar **80-pillared hall**; Bulandibagh timber palisade; NBPW; punch-marked coins.
- **Sudarshana lake (Girnar):** Pushyagupta builds → **Tushaspha** canals (Ashoka) → **Rudradaman** repairs (150 CE) → **Skandagupta** (456 CE).

---

## 3. Chandragupta and Bindusara

- **Chandragupta (c. 321–297 BCE):** ousts Dhana Nanda with Chanakya; Greek **Sandrocottus** (identified by William Jones, 1793).
- **c. 305 BCE vs Seleucus Nicator:** gains **Arachosia, Gedrosia, Paropamisadae**; gives **500 elephants**; Megasthenes sent.
- Jain end: with **Bhadrabahu**, **sallekhana** at **Shravanabelagola**.
- **Bindusara (c. 297–273):** **Amitraghata** (Greek Amitrochates); Deccan conquests; Ajivika patron; Greek envoy **Deimachus**. Asked Antiochus I for sweet wine, figs and a philosopher.

## 4. Ashoka and dhamma

- Viceroy at **Taxila** and **Ujjayini**; crowned c. **268 BCE**; titles **Devanampiya Piyadasi** (1995: Priyadarshi).
- **Kalinga war** c. **261 BCE** (8th regnal year); Kalinga controlled routes south (2000); RE XIII: **100,000 killed, 150,000 deported**.
- Lay Buddhist (**upasaka**, Maski MRE); **Upagupta** (tradition); **dhamma-yatras** replace vihara-yatras; **3rd council** (Pataliputra, Moggaliputta Tissa); **Mahinda, Sanghamitra** to Sri Lanka; **Barabar caves** to Ajivikas.
- **Dhamma** = obedience to elders, respect for Brahmanas and Shramanas, kindness to slaves, ahimsa to animals, **tolerance**, welfare. An **ethic, not Buddhism** (1998: social harmony over devotion).
- Tools: **dhamma-mahamattas** (RE V), welfare (RE II), no sacrifice at festivals (RE I). Thapar: an ideology to unite a diverse empire.

## 5. The edicts

- **Scripts:** Prakrit in **Brahmi** (most); **Kharoshthi**, right → left (**Shahbazgarhi, Mansehra**); **Greek + Aramaic** (**Kandahar**).
- **Decipherment:** **James Prinsep, 1837** (2016).
- **14 Major Rock Edicts:** **Kalsi** (Uttarakhand) · **Girnar** (Gujarat) · **Sopara** (Maharashtra) · **Dhauli, Jaugada** (Odisha) · **Erragudi** (AP) · **Sannati** (Karnataka) · Shahbazgarhi, Mansehra (Pakistan) · Kandahar (Afghanistan).
- **2022:** Dhauli–Odisha ✓, Erragudi–AP ✓, Jaugada–MP ✗ (Odisha), Kalsi–Karnataka ✗ (Uttarakhand) → **only two**.
- **Kalinga Separate Edicts** (Dhauli, Jaugada) replace RE XI–XIII: "**All men are my children**".
- **Minor RE:** Maski, Brahmagiri, Rupnath, Sasaram, Bairat (Bhabru), Gujarra, Nittur, Udegolam. Name **"Asoka"**: **Maski, Gujarra, Nittur, Udegolam** (1997: Maski).
- **7 Pillar Edicts:** all 7 only on **Delhi–Topra**; also Delhi–Meerut, Allahabad (from Kaushambi), Lauriya-Araraj, Lauriya-Nandangarh, Rampurva.
- **Minor pillar:** **Rummindei** (Lumbini: **bali removed, bhaga cut to 1/8**) · Nigali Sagar · **Schism Edict** (Sarnath, Sanchi, Allahabad) · **Queen's Edict** (Allahabad: **Karuvaki**, son **Tivara**).
- **Kanganahalli** (near Sannati, KA): relief with **"Ranyo Ashoka"** — only labelled portrait (2019).

## 6. Administration and economy

- **Ladder:** King (mantriparishad) → **provinces** under kumaras (**Taxila**–Uttarapatha, **Ujjayini**–Avanti, **Tosali**–Kalinga, **Suvarnagiri**–Dakshinapatha, Pataliputra centre) → **district: pradeshika, rajuka, yukta (2025)** → village: **gramika**.
- **Saptanga:** swami, amatya, janapada, durga, kosha, danda, mitra ("Some Able Judges Defend Kings During Monsoon").
- **Mandala:** neighbour = natural enemy; neighbour's neighbour = friend.
- **Officers:** **samaharta** (revenue), **sannidhata** (treasury), **adhyakshas** (sita-, panya-, shulka-, loha-, ganika-adhyaksha…); **gudhapurushas** (spies).
- **Slavery (2022, UPSC key 2 and 3):** female slave bearing master's son → **free**; son gets **master's son's** status.
- **Revenue:** **bhaga** (usually 1/6), **bali**, **sita** (crown land). **Punch-marked** silver pana/karshapana; copper mashaka; monopolies (mines, salt).
- **Uttarapatha** (Taxila–Pataliputra) → Grand Trunk Road precursor.
- **Megasthenes:** 6 city boards × 5 (crafts, foreigners, **births & deaths**, trade, goods, 1/10 tax); army 6 boards; **570 towers, 64 gates**; claims 7 castes, no slavery (wrong).

## 7. Mauryan art

- **Court art:** pillars · Kumrahar palace · **Barabar caves** (Lomas Rishi, Sudama; Ajivikas) · **Nagarjuni** (Dasharatha) · stupas (84,000 legend; Sanchi, Bharhut cores).
- **Popular art:** **Parkham Yaksha** (Mathura) · **Besnagar Yakshi** · **Didarganj Yakshi** (chauri-bearer, Patna; polished; date debated) · terracotta.
- **Sarnath capital (top → bottom):** 4 lions back to back (once crowned by a wheel) → **abacus: elephant, bull, horse, lion + 24-spoke wheels** → **inverted-lotus bell** → **monolithic shaft, no base**; **Chunar sandstone**; **Mauryan polish**.
- **State Emblem 26 Jan 1950** (Satyameva Jayate); 6.5 m bronze on new Parliament, July 2022.
- **Other capitals:** **Rampurva bull** (+ lion) · **Sankissa elephant** · **Lauriya-Nandangarh** single lion · **Vaishali (Kolhua)** single lion, uninscribed · Sanchi four lions · Dhauli rock-cut elephant.
- **vs Persian (Achaemenid):** Persian = segmented, fluted, on a base, roof-bearing. Ashokan = monolithic, plain, baseless, free-standing → influence, not copying.
- **Lomas Rishi:** timber hut façade in stone, arch + elephant frieze → chaitya façades (Karle, Ajanta).
- **Firoz Shah Tughlaq** moved Topra and Meerut pillars to Delhi (1350s; 42-wheeled cart).

## 8. Early Buddhist stupa art (Mains 2016)

- **Bharhut** (MP, c. 2nd c. BCE, Sunga; labelled panels in Brahmi; Indian Museum, Kolkata) · **Sanchi** toranas (1st c. BCE; **ivory-carvers of Vidisha** inscription) · later Amaravati.
- **Stupa:** anda → harmika → chhatra; vedika; toranas; pradakshina path.
- **Aniconic Buddha:** footprints, empty throne, Bodhi tree, wheel, stupa, riderless horse.
- **Folk motifs:** yakshas, yakshis (**shalabhanjika**), nagas, animals, lotus vines.
- **Narratives:** Jatakas (**Chhaddanta**, six-tusked elephant), life scenes; continuous narration.
- **Donors:** monks, nuns, merchants, guilds.
- Line: folk motifs guard, Jatakas teach, symbols make the absent Buddha present.

## 9. Decline

- Weak successors (Dasharatha, Samprati…), possible partition; over-centralisation; financial strain; NW neglect (Indo-Greeks).
- Doubted theories: Brahmanical reaction (H. P. Shastri); pacifism (H. C. Raychaudhuri).
- **c. 185 BCE: Pushyamitra Sunga kills Brihadratha** at an army review.
