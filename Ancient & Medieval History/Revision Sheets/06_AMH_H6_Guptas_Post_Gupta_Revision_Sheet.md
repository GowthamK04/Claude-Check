## 2. The Gupta chain ("Can Samudra Cross Kings' Seas?")

- **Chandragupta I (c. 319–335):** **Gupta era 320 CE**; married **Kumaradevi** (Lichchhavi) → King–Queen coins; **Maharajadhiraja**.
- **Samudragupta (c. 335–375):** **Allahabad Prashasti** by **Harishena** on the **Ashokan pillar** (2006). Policies: Aryavarta — 9 kings **uprooted** · Dakshinapatha — 12 kings **captured–released–reinstated** (*grahana–moksha–anugraha*) · forest (atavika) kings made servants · frontier kings and foreigners pay tribute; **Meghavarna** (Sri Lanka) builds a Bodh Gaya monastery. **Veena** coins, Ashvamedha, *Kaviraja*, "Napoleon of India" (V. A. Smith).
- **Ramagupta:** disputed (*Devichandraguptam*).
- **Chandragupta II Vikramaditya (c. 375–415):** ends Western Kshatrapas (**Rudrasimha III**); **Ujjayini** second capital; **silver** coins; daughter **Prabhavati Gupta** → Vakataka Rudrasena II; **Fa-hien** (2025); **Udayagiri** (Varaha); **Mehrauli iron pillar** ("Chandra"; rust-free, phosphorus-rich iron); Kalidasa (navaratna legend).
- **Kumaragupta I (c. 415–455):** founded **Nalanda** ("Shakraditya"); peacock/Kartikeya coins; Huns appear.
- **Skandagupta (c. 455–467):** repelled **Huns** (**Bhitari** pillar); **Junagadh**: Sudarshana repaired 456 by **Parnadatta** / Chakrapalita; coins debased.
- **End:** Toramana, **Mihirakula**; **Eran inscription of Bhanugupta, 510 CE — first sati** (Goparaja's wife); Mihirakula beaten by **Yashodharman of Mandasor** (532) and Narasimhagupta Baladitya; empire gone by c. 550.

## 3. State, land, economy

- **Titles:** Maharajadhiraja, Paramabhattaraka, Parameshvara; divine kingship; **Garuda** emblem.
- **Officials:** kumaramatya; **sandhivigrahika** (peace & war — Harishena); mahadandanayaka; offices **hereditary**.
- **Units:** **bhukti** (uparika) → **vishaya** (vishayapati) → **vithi** → **grama** (gramika).
- **Taxes:** bhaga (1/6–1/4), bhoga, uparikara, udranga; **vishti = forced labour as tax** (2019).
- **Land grants:** **agrahara** (tax-free, with admin rights) on copper plates (Damodarpur); **kulyavapa, dronavapa = land measures** (2020).
- **Coins:** gold **dinara** (suvarna) · silver **rupaka** (1997) · little copper.
- **8 coin types:** Archer · King–Queen · Lyrist (veena) · Ashvamedha · Lion-slayer · Tiger-slayer · Horseman · Peacock (Kartikeya).
- **Ports:** **Tamralipti** (1999) · **Ghantasala, Kadura, Chaul** (2020) · Broach.
- Trade turns to **Southeast Asia** after Rome (1999); guilds (Mandasor silk-weavers' Sun temple, 436); **Hundi = bill of exchange** (2020, post-Harsha).

## 4. Society and culture

- Rigid varna; **chandalas** outside towns, striking wood (Fa-hien); women speak **Prakrit** in plays (1995); **Eran sati 510**; **Prabhavati Gupta** regent; Narada and Brihaspati smritis.
- Religion: **Bhagavatism** (Paramabhagavata), Shaivism, Puranas finalised; Buddhism (Nalanda, Sarnath Buddha, Sultanganj Buddha); Jain council at Valabhi.
- **Kalidasa:** Abhijnanashakuntalam, Malavikagnimitram, Vikramorvashiyam · Meghaduta, Raghuvamsha, Kumarasambhava, Ritusamhara.
- **Shudraka** — Mricchakatika · **Vishakhadatta** — Mudrarakshasa, Devichandraguptam · **Amarasimha** — Amarakosha · **Vishnu Sharma** — Panchatantra · Aryabhata (499), Varahamihira (H9) · 1996 key: **Sushruta** for medicine.
- **Temples:** Sanchi 17 (flat roof) · Tigawa · **Dashavatara, Deogarh** (early shikhara) · **Bhitargaon** (brick) · Nachna Parvati.
- **Art:** **Udayagiri** Varaha · Sarnath Buddha · **Ajanta 16–17** (Vakataka, Harishena) · Bagh.
- **Nalanda vs Taxila (2014 adj.):** Nalanda residential, entrance tests, Dharmaganja library, village grants, foreign students → university; Taxila independent teachers. Nalanda sacked c. 1193; refounded 2010; new campus 2024.

## 5. Post-Gupta north and Harsha

- **c. 550–606 (2021):** **Later Guptas** (Magadha) · **Maukharis** (Kannauj) · **Pushyabhutis** (Thanesar) · **Maitrakas** (Valabhi) · Gauda (**Shashanka**) — not Paramaras, not Yadavas.
- **Harsha (606–647):** brother Rajyavardhana killed by Shashanka; sister **Rajyashri** (Maukhari widow); **Thanesar → Kannauj**; stopped by **Pulakeshin II on the Narmada** (c. 618–19, Aihole) (2003); **Kannauj assembly 643** (Mahayana, Hiuen Tsang); **Prayag** every five years — charity to all (2001: A true, R false); plays **Nagananda, Ratnavali, Priyadarshika**; **Banabhatta** (Harshacharita, Kadambari); Madhuban, Banskhera plates.

## 6. Chalukyas of Badami (c. 543–753)

- **Pulakeshin I:** Badami fort; inscription Saka 465 = **543 CE** (1997).
- **Pulakeshin II (610–642):** Dakshinapatheshvara; beat **Harsha**; **Aihole inscription 634** by **Ravikirti** (Jain; mentions **Kalidasa, Bharavi**); Persian embassy (Khusrau II); killed 642 (Vatapikonda).
- **Vikramaditya II:** took Kanchi; queen **Lokamahadevi** — **Virupaksha, Pattadakal** (copies Kailasanatha).
- **End:** **Dantidurga** (Rashtrakuta), 753.
- **Art:** **Aihole** (Lad Khan, Durga) · **Badami** caves · **Pattadakal** — Nagara (Papanatha) + Dravida (Virupaksha, Mallikarjuna); UNESCO 1987.
- Eastern Chalukyas of Vengi (624, Kubja Vishnuvardhana).

## 7. Pallavas of Kanchi (c. 575–897)

- **Simhavishnu:** Bharavi (Kiratarjuniya).
- **Mahendravarman I (600–630):** **Mattavilasa, Vichitrachitta, Gunabhara** (2025); Chitrakarapuli; **Mattavilasa-prahasana**; Jain → Shaiva (**Appar**); rock-cut (Mandagapattu, Tiruchirappalli).
- **Narasimhavarman I "Mamalla" (630–668):** **Vatapikonda** 642; naval aid to Manavarma (Sri Lanka); Hiuen Tsang at Kanchi; rathas.
- **Narasimhavarman II Rajasimha (695–722):** **Shore Temple**, **Kailasanatha (Kanchi)**; **Dandin** (Dashakumaracharita, Kavyadarsha).
- **Nandivarman II:** Vaikunthaperumal.
- **Ghatikas** at Kanchi (2016); Alvars, Nayanars (H11); ended by Chola **Aditya I** (c. 897).

### Pallava art — four styles (2024)

| Style | King | Monuments |
|---|---|---|
| Mahendra | Mahendravarman I | rock-cut mandapas (Mandagapattu: no brick, timber, metal, mortar) |
| Mamalla | Narasimhavarman I | **Pancha Pandava rathas**, **Arjuna's Penance / Descent of the Ganga** (Mamallapuram, UNESCO 1984) |
| Rajasimha | Narasimhavarman II | **structural**: Shore Temple, Kailasanatha |
| Nandivarman | Nandivarman II | Vaikunthaperumal, Muktesvara |

- **Duel:** Pulakeshin II beats Mahendravarman (Pullalur) → 634 Aihole → 642 Vatapikonda → Vikramaditya II takes Kanchi → 753 Dantidurga.

## 8. Feudalism (R. S. Sharma)

- Land grants with rights → **samantas** → vishti, tied peasants → fewer coins, declining towns → self-sufficient villages.
- Critics: Harbans Mukhia, B. D. Chattopadhyaya (no serfdom; new towns).
- 2000 A–R: feudalism traced to military campaigns; expanded under Guptas → **(b)** both true, R not the explanation.
