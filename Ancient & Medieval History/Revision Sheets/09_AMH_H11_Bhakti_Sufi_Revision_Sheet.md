## The journey in ten lines

1. **6th–9th c.** — 12 **Alvars** (Vishnu) and 63 **Nayanars** (Shiva) sing in **Tamil**, from every caste, women included (Andal).
2. **Shankara** (Advaita: the world is maya) is answered by **Ramanuja** (Vishishtadvaita: **devotion** saves) — then Nimbarka, Madhva, Vallabha.
3. **12th c.** — **Basavanna** at Kalyana founds the **Lingayats**: no caste, no rebirth, the ishtalinga; the Tamil **Siddhas** reject idols.
4. **13th–17th c.** — Maharashtra's **Varkaris** walk to **Pandharpur**: Jnaneshwar, **Namdev the tailor**, Eknath, Tukaram.
5. **15th c.** — **Ramananda** preaches in **Hindi** to all castes; his pupils include **Kabir** (weaver), **Ravidas** (cobbler), **Sena** (barber).
6. **Nirguna** (Kabir, Ravidas, **Nanak**, Dadu) vs **saguna** (Tulsidas, Surdas, **Mirabai**).
7. **Guru Nanak** (1469–1539) preaches as the Lodis fall (1526); Sikhism begins.
8. **East** — **Chaitanya** turns bhakti public with **sankirtan** and Radha–Krishna; **Shankaradeva** builds **satras** in Assam.
9. **From 1192** — the **Sufis**: **Muinuddin Chishti** at Ajmer → Nizamuddin in Delhi; other orders (Suhrawardi, Qadiri, Naqshbandi).
10. **Where they met** — vernacular songs, shared shrines, Kabir and Baba Farid in one Granth; **caste survived, but the heart of religion changed.**

## 2. Tamil roots (6th–9th c.)

- **12 Alvars** (Vishnu) · **63 Nayanars** (Shiva) · Tamil · all castes, women.
- **Andal** (only woman Alvar; "married" Ranganatha of Srirangam) · **Tiruppan Alvar** (low caste) · **Karaikkal Ammaiyar** · **Nandanar** (untouchable Nayanar) · **Appar** (converted Pallava Mahendravarman I), **Sambandar**, **Sundarar**, **Manikkavachakar** (Tiruvachakam).
- **Nalayira Divya Prabandham** — compiled by **Nathamuni** (10th c.), "Tamil Veda" · **Tevaram** → **Tirumurai** (Chola era).
- Opposed Jains and Buddhists.

## 3. The acharyas ("Some Real Nice Mystics Veil")

| Acharya | Dates | Place | School | Key idea |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Shankara** | c. 788–820 | Kaladi (Kerala) | **Advaita** | soul = Brahman; world = maya; knowledge; mathas Sringeri, Dwarka, Puri, Jyotirmath |
| **Ramanuja** | c. 1017–1137 | Sriperumbudur; Srirangam | **Vishishtadvaita** | qualified non-dualism; **devotion best means** (2022) |
| **Nimbarka** | c. 12th–13th c. | Vrindavan | **Dvaitadvaita** | Radha–Krishna; **not** Akbar's contemporary (2019) |
| **Madhva** | 1238–1317 | Udupi | **Dvaita** | God and soul separate; inspired Haridasas |
| **Vallabha** | 1479–1531 | Nathdwara | **Shuddhadvaita** | **Pushtimarga** (grace); child Krishna (Srinathji); pupil **Surdas** |

- **Statue of Equality** (Ramanuja), 216 ft seated, near Hyderabad — **Feb 2022**.
- **Haridasas** (Karnataka, Madhva line): **Purandaradasa** ("father of Carnatic music"), **Kanakadasa**.

## 4. Rebels: Lingayats and Siddhas

- **Basavanna** (12th c.): minister of Kalachuri **Bijjala** at **Kalyana** · **Virashaiva/Lingayat** · **ishtalinga** · **Anubhava Mantapa** · **vachanas** (Kannada) · Akka Mahadevi, Allama Prabhu · "kayakave kailasa" (work is worship).
- Lingayats: **rejected caste**, **questioned rebirth**, allowed widow remarriage (2016 ✓).
- Karnataka cabinet declared Basavanna the state's **"cultural leader" — 18 Jan 2024**.
- **Siddhas/Sittars** (Tamil): **monotheistic**, **condemned idolatry** and caste (2016 ✓) → 2016 answer **(c) both**.

## 6. The north

- **Ramananda** — **first to preach bhakti in Hindi** (2002); disciples of all castes.
- **2001 professions:** **Namdev — tailor · Kabir — weaver · Ravidas — cobbler · Sena — barber** (also Dhanna — Jat peasant).

| Nirguna (formless God) | Saguna (God with form) |
|---|---|
| rejects idols, caste, ritual, pilgrimage | Rama/Krishna with stories, temples |
| **Kabir** (Bijak, dohas) · **Ravidas** · **Nanak** · **Dadu Dayal** (Akbar's time) | **Tulsidas** (Ramcharitmanas, Awadhi) · **Surdas** (Sursagar, Braj) · **Mirabai** (Krishna; Merta/Mewar) |

- Kabir **not** influenced by **Ahmad Sirhindi** (born 1564, after Kabir) (2019).
- Kabir legend: Hindus and Muslims quarrel over his body; flowers under the sheet.
- **Guru Nanak** (1469–1539): Talwandi → **Kartarpur** · one God · **langar** · honest work · preaching when **Lodis fell (1526)** (2013 — only Nanak; Dadu 16th c., Tyagaraja 18th–19th c.).
- **Kartarpur Corridor** opened **Nov 2019**.
- **Sikh Gurus (2004):** Nanak → **Angad** (Gurmukhi — standard view; UPSC key accepted Arjan) → Amar Das → Ram Das (Amritsar) → **Arjan** (Adi Granth 1604; after Ram Das ✓) … → **Gobind Singh** sent **Banda Bahadur** (not Tegh Bahadur ✗) → answer **(b) 2 and 3**.

## 7. The east

- **Chaitanya Mahaprabhu** (1486–1534): born **Nabadwip** (Vishvambhar/Nimai) · **Radha–Krishna** · **sankirtan** (public, collective) · open to all castes, some Muslims · **achintya-bhedabheda** · **Six Goswamis** of **Vrindavan** · last years at **Puri** · **Gaudiya Vaishnavism** → ISKCON.
- **2018 re-orientation:** private/literary (Jayadeva, Chandidas) → public celebration; Radha's longing central; caste no bar; written theology; Bengal–Puri–Vrindavan network; limit: saguna, Brahmanical forms kept.
- **Shankaradeva** (Assam, 1449–1568): **Ekasarana Dharma** · **namghar** · **satras** (Majuli) · **Borgeet** · **Ankiya Naat** → **Sattriya** (classical, 2000).

## 8. Sufism

- **Vocabulary:** pir/shaikh · murid · **silsila** · **khanqah** · **dargah** · **sama** (music, qawwali) · **zikr** · **urs** · langar · **fana** · **wahdat-ul-wujud** (Ibn Arabi) vs **wahdat-ul-shuhud** (Sirhindi) · ba-shara vs be-shara.
- **2012:** breath control ✓ · solitary austerities ✓ · ecstatic songs ✓ → **all three**.
- **Chishti chain:** **Muinuddin** (Ajmer, c. 1192; **music brings one close to God** — 1996) → **Bakhtiyar Kaki** (Delhi) → **Baba Farid** (Pakpattan; verses in Guru Granth) → **Nizamuddin Auliya** (Delhi; seven sultans; Amir Khusrau; "two doors") → **Nasiruddin Chiragh-i-Dehli** (his disciple — 2002 ✓) → **Gesudaraz** (Gulbarga) · **Salim Chishti** (Sikri; Akbar — not Aurangzeb, 2002).

## 9. Impact, literature, limits

- **Meeting points:** vernacular songs; shared shrines; **Guru Granth Sahib** holds Kabir, Namdev, Ravidas, **Baba Farid**.
- **Literature (2021):** Tamil hymns · Kannada vachanas · Marathi Jnaneshwari, abhangs · Hindi dohas, Ramcharitmanas, Sursagar · Rajasthani Mira · Bengali padavali · Assamese Borgeet · Punjabi Adi Granth → regional languages, music (kirtan, abhang, qawwali, Carnatic), theatre (Ankiya Naat), dignity for low castes and women, syncretism.
- **Limits (2014):** caste survived; sects became castes (Kabirpanthis, Lingayats); temples and Brahmanical authority stayed; orthodoxy (ulema, Sirhindi) resisted.
- **Verdict:** changed the **inner** life of religion more than its **outer** structure.
