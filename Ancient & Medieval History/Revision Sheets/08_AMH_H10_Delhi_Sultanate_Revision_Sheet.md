## The whole story in ten lines

1. **1192** — Muhammad of Ghor beats Prithviraj at **Tarain**; his slave **Aibak** stays on and becomes sultan in **1206**.
2. **Iltutmish** turns a conquest into a state: Delhi capital, **the Forty**, **iqtas**, **tanka/jital** — and keeps **Chengiz Khan** out (1221).
3. **Razia** rules and falls; **Balban** tames the nobles with awe: **Zil-i-Ilahi**, sijda, paibos.
4. **1290** — the **Khalji revolution**: power opens beyond Turkish birth.
5. **Alauddin** faces Mongols at Delhi (1299, **1303**) → **half-produce revenue + fixed prices + cash army**; **Malik Kafur** reaches Madurai (1311).
6. **Muhammad bin Tughlaq**'s five experiments (Daulatabad, token coins…) overstretch the empire → **Vijayanagara (1336)**, **Bahmani (1347)** break away.
7. **Firuz** soothes: canals, charity, slaves' department, Ashokan pillars moved to Delhi.
8. **1398** — **Timur** sacks Delhi under the last Tughlaq, **Nasiruddin Mahmud**.
9. **Sayyids** (cap-wearers) and **Lodis** (Bahlol → Sikandar → Ibrahim) rule a smaller kingdom; Sikandar founds **Agra** (1504).
10. **1526** — **Babur**, heir of Timur and Chengiz, wins at **Panipat**. The Sultanate leaves behind the **Persian wheel, paper, the true arch and Hindavi**.

## 2. Mamluks / Slave dynasty (1206–1290)

- **Aibak (1206–10):** *Lakh-bakhsh* · **Quwwat-ul-Islam**, **Qutb Minar** begun, **Adhai Din ka Jhonpra** (Ajmer) · died falling from his horse playing **chaugan** (2003).
- **Iltutmish (1211–36):** real founder · capital **Delhi** · **Turkan-i-Chahalgani** (the Forty) · **iqta** · silver **tanka**, copper **jital** · caliph's investiture **1229** · refused Jalaluddin Mangbarni → **Chengiz Khan turned back at the Indus, 1221** (2021, 2001) · completed Qutb Minar.
- **Razia (1236–40):** first woman ruler · Yaqut · overthrown by the Forty.
- **Balban (1266–87):** **Zil-i-Ilahi** ("Shadow of God", 1997) · **sijda**, **paibos**, Nauroz · Afrasiyab descent · broke the Forty · **Diwan-i-Arz** · spies (barids) · son killed by Mongols (1285) · tomb: first **true arch**.

---

## 3. Khaljis (1290–1320)

- **Khalji revolution** (1290): non-Turks rise. Jalaluddin killed by nephew **Alauddin** (1296), rich from **Devagiri**.
- **Conquests:** Gujarat & **Somnath 1299** · **Ranthambore 1301** · **Chittor 1303** (Padmavati legend — Jayasi, 1540) · Malwa.
- **Malik Kafur (1307–11):** Devagiri → Warangal → Dwarasamudra → **Madurai** (1995 map).
- **Mongols** besieged Delhi **1299** and **1303** (Targhi) (2022 ✓).
- **Reforms (one system):**
  - Revenue: land **measured**; **kharaj = ½ produce** (Doab), in kind; rural chiefs' privileges cut; **Diwan-i-Mustakhraj** (arrears, 2001).
  - Market: **fixed prices** · **shahna-i-mandi** · **Diwan-i-Riyasat** · granaries · **Banjaras** (grain-carrying traders, 2016) · spies · harsh punishments.
  - Army: standing army paid in **cash** · **dagh** (branding horses) · **chehra** (descriptive roll) — Alauddin, not Balban (2002).
  - Logic: cheap grain + fixed prices → low cash salaries → large army vs Mongols.
- **Alai Darwaza (1311):** first **true dome** · Siri fort · Hauz-i-Alai · **Amir Khusrau** (*Khazain-ul-Futuh*).
- Collapsed after his death (1316): Malik Kafur blinds heirs, is killed.

## 4. Tughlaqs (1320–1414)

- **Ghiyasuddin (1320–25):** **Tughlaqabad** (battered walls) · killed in pavilion collapse.
- **Muhammad bin Tughlaq (1325–51) — five experiments:**
  1. Capital → **Daulatabad** (Devagiri), 1327; back again.
  2. **Token currency** (copper/brass), c. 1329–30; forged; withdrawn. (Gold **dinar** was real — 2006.)
  3. **Doab tax** raised during famine → revolt.
  4. **Diwan-i-Kohi** (agriculture; state loans) — MbT, not Alauddin (2001).
  5. **Khurasan** plan; **Qarachil** (Kumaon) expedition disaster.
- **Swargadwari** camp during plague (2002 A–R (a)) · **Ibn Battuta** (arrived 1333; qazi; to China 1342; *Rihla*).
- Breakaways: **Vijayanagara 1336**, **Bahmani 1347** (H12) · died at **Thatta 1351** · Badauni: "the king was freed from his people and they from their king" (1999).
- **Firuz (1351–88):** largest **canal** network (1998; Western Yamuna) · **Diwan-i-Bandagan** (slaves; 2002, 2001) · **Diwan-i-Khairat** · **Dar-ul-Shifa** · employment bureau · **Firozabad/Kotla** · repaired Qutb Minar · moved **Topra & Meerut Ashokan pillars** · hereditary iqtas · only 4 sharia taxes · **jizya on Brahmanas** · *Futuhat-i-Firuzshahi*.
- **Timur 1398** under **Nasiruddin Mahmud**, last Tughlaq (2004) — not MbT (2021 ✗).

## 5. Sayyids & Lodis (1414–1526)

- **Sayyids (1414–51):** **Khizr Khan** (Timur's governor) · **Kulah-daran** = "cap-wearers" (2022).
- **Lodis (1451–1526), first Afghans** — order **Bahlol → Sikandar → Ibrahim** (2006):
  - **Bahlol:** annexed **Jaunpur** (Sharqis) (1999 map).
  - **Sikandar:** founded **Agra (1504)** · **gaz-i-Sikandari** · pen-name "Gulrukhi" · tomb with early **double dome**.
  - **Ibrahim:** uncle **Alam Khan** (pretender, 2003) and **Daulat Khan Lodi** invite Babur → **Panipat, 21 April 1526**.

## 6. Administration

- Sultan + caliph's nominal authority + **sharia**; sultans' own laws = **zawabit** → **Barani**: not truly Islamic (2002) · *Fatawa-i-Jahandari*.
- **Iqta** (Islamic origin; not indigenous — 2019) · holder **muqti/iqtadar** · surplus **fawazil** to treasury (1998) · early rule military because land parcelled as iqtas (1998 A–R (a)).
- **Amil** = revenue collector (2019 ✓) · **Mir Bakshi** = Mughal (2019 ✗).
- **Taxes:** **kharaj** (land) · **jizya** (non-Muslims) · **khams** (⅕ booty) · **zakat** (Muslim alms).

## 7. Technology, culture, architecture (Mains 2023)

| Technology | Changed |
|---|---|
| **Persian wheel** (saqiya/rahat) | more irrigation, second crops |
| **Spinning wheel** (charkha) | textile boom; women's household income |
| **Carding bow** | cheaper cloth; carders (dhunia) |
| **Paper** (13th c.) | records, chronicles, bureaucracy |
| **True arch & dome**, lime mortar | new skyline |
| **Stirrup, iron horseshoe** | Turkish cavalry edge |
| **Sericulture** | local silk (Bengal) |
| Distillation, glazed tiles | urban crafts |

- **Society impact:** agrarian expansion · urbanisation and markets · new artisan groups · Persian-literate bureaucracy · Indo-Islamic architecture.
- **Skyline:** corbelled Quwwat screen (1190s) → **Balban's tomb: first true arch** → **Alai Darwaza 1311: first true dome** → **Tughlaqabad: battered walls** → Firuz's Kotla, Hauz Khas → **Lodi: double dome**, octagonal tombs · **Qutb Minar** (~73 m) UNESCO 1993.
- **1998 order:** Qutb Minar → Tughlaqabad → Lodi Garden → Fatehpur Sikri.
- **Writers:** **Amir Khusrau** (Hindavi, qawwali, *Tuti-i-Hind*) · **Minhaj-us-Siraj** (*Tabaqat-i-Nasiri*) · **Barani** (*Tarikh-i-Firuzshahi*) · **Afif** (*Tarikh-i-Firuzshahi*) · **Isami** (*Futuh-us-Salatin*) · **Ibn Battuta** (*Rihla*).
