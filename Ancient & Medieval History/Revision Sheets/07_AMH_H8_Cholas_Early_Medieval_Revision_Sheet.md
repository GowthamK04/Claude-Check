## 2. A new age

- Regional kingdoms; land grants and **samantas**; temples as economic hubs; regional languages.
- **2015 feudal essentials:** administration based on **control of land** + **lord–vassal** ties; **not** a strong centre → answer (b) 2 and 3.
- **712:** Muhammad bin Qasim takes Sindh from Dahir (*Chachnama*).
- **Mahmud of Ghazni:** 17 raids (1000–1027); **Somnath 1025**; Kannauj 1018; **Al-Biruni** — *Kitab-ul-Hind*.
- **1999:** the south had regional kingdoms because it lacked **vast fertile plains**.

## 3. Tripartite struggle for Kannauj (c. 750–1000)

- **Palas (Bengal–Bihar):** **Gopala** (c. 750) **elected** to end *matsyanyaya* (Khalimpur plate) · **Dharmapala** — **Vikramashila**, **Somapura** (Paharpur, UNESCO 1985) · **Devapala** — 5 villages to **Nalanda** for Sailendra king **Balaputradeva** · Mahipala I beaten by Rajendra Chola · Ramapala (*Ramacharita*, Sandhyakara Nandi) → Senas (Jayadeva). Buddhist patrons (2006 ✓); **Vajrayana**; Odantapuri; **Atisha Dipankara → Tibet 1042**; Pala bronzes (Kurkihar), palm-leaf manuscripts.
- **Gurjara-Pratiharas:** **Nagabhata I** stops Arabs · **Nagabhata II** takes Kannauj (2022 ✓) · **Mihira Bhoja (836–885)** — "Adivaraha"; **Sulaiman (851)**: best cavalry · **Mahendrapala I** — poet **Rajashekhara** (Karpuramanjari, Kavyamimamsa) · fade after Mahmud (1018).
- **Rashtrakutas:** **Dantidurga** founder, 753 (2006) · **Krishna I** — **Kailasa, Ellora (Cave 16)**, carved top-down · Dhruva, Govinda III raid Kannauj · **Amoghavarsha I (814–878)** — **Manyakheta**, *Kavirajamarga*, Jain (Jinasena), one of the world's "four great kings" (Sulaiman) · **Krishna III** — **Takkolam 949** over Cholas · ended 973 (Tailapa II).
- **Result:** Pratiharas held Kannauj longest; all three exhausted → Rajputs, Turks, Cholas.
- **2020 order:** Pallava Mahendravarman I (600–630) → Pala Gopala (c. 750) → Pratihara Bhoja (836) → Chola Parantaka I (907) = **2-4-1-3**.

## 6. The Cholas (c. 850–1279)

- **Vijayalaya** (c. 850) — Thanjavur from Muttaraiyars.
- **Aditya I** — ended the Pallavas (Aparajita, c. 897).
- **Parantaka I (907–955)** — **Maduraikonda**; **Uttaramerur inscriptions 919, 921**; lost at **Takkolam 949**.
- **Rajaraja I (985–1014)** — Kandalur Salai; **northern Sri Lanka** (2001 ✓; Anuradhapura → Polonnaruwa); **Maldives**; land survey; **Brihadeeswara (1010)**.
- **Rajendra I (1014–1044)** — all Sri Lanka; Ganga expedition beats **Mahipala I** (1022–23) → **Gangaikondachola**, **Gangaikondacholapuram**; **Srivijaya raid 1025** → **Kadaram-konda** (2025 ✓; 2003 ✓ "Sailendra").
- **Kulottunga I (1070–1122)** — Chola + Eastern Chalukya (Vengi); **72 merchants to China (1077)**; tolls abolished (*sungam tavirtta*).
- **Rajaraja II** — **Airavatesvara, Darasuram** · **Kulottunga III** — Tribhuvanam.
- **End:** Rajendra III (1279) → Pandyas.
- Mnemonic: "**V**ery **A**ble **P**rinces **R**ule **R**ight, **K**indly".

### Administration and economy

- **King** → **mandalam** → **valanadu** → **nadu** → village.
- Assemblies: **ur** (non-Brahmana villages) · **sabha/mahasabha** (Brahmana villages) · **nagaram** (merchants).
- **Variyams** (committees): eri-variyam (tank), totta-variyam (garden), samvatsara-variyam (annual), pon-variyam (gold), justice.
- **Kudavolai** (Uttaramerur): names on palm-leaf tickets → pot → a boy draws one per ward → one-year term. Eligible: **35–70**, owns land and house, Vedic learning. Barred: served in last 3 years, failed to submit accounts, relatives of offenders.
- **Land:** vellanvagai · **brahmadeya** · **devadana** · **shalabhoga** · pallichchhandam · **eripatti** (tank upkeep; 2016 ✓).
- **2016:** eripatti ✓, **taniyur** = large separate village (not a Brahmana gift) ✗, **ghatika** = temple college ✓ → **(d) 1 and 3**.
- Revenue up to **one-third**; temples as landlords, banks, employers.
- Guilds: **Ainnurruvar** (Five Hundred of Ayyavole), **Manigramam** (1997 ✓), Nanadesi; port **Nagapattinam**; army Kaikolar, Velaikkarar; navy.

### Art and architecture (Mains 2013, 2022, 2024)

- **Evolution:** Pallava roots → early Chola (**Vijayalaya Cholishvaram**, Narttamalai; **Koranganatha**, Srinivasanallur; Nageshvara) → **Brihadeeswara, Thanjavur** (Rajaraja I, 1010; ~66 m, 13-storey granite vimana; capstone ~80 t) → **Gangaikondacholapuram** (Rajendra I) → **Airavatesvara, Darasuram** (Rajaraja II) → after Cholas, gopurams dominate.
- Parts: gopuram → Nandi mandapa → pillared mandapas → antarala → **garbhagriha** under **vimana** → shikhara → kalasha.
- **UNESCO:** Great Living Chola Temples — Thanjavur **1987**, + Gangaikondacholapuram & Darasuram **2004**.
- **Bronzes:** **lost-wax**; **Nataraja** (ananda tandava, ring of fire, **Chidambaram**); Somaskanda, Parvati.
- **Frescoes** (rediscovered 1931); **81 of 108 karanas** carved.
- **Tamil literature:** **Kamban** — Ramavataram · **Sekkilar** — Periyapuranam (63 Nayanars) · **Jayamkondar** — Kalingattupparani · Ottakoothar · **Nambi Andar Nambi** — Tirumurai.

## 7. Society, law, literature

| | Mitakshara | Dayabhaga |
|---|---|---|
| Author | **Vijnaneshvara** (Kalyani court; on Yajnavalkya Smriti) | **Jimutavahana** (Bengal, 12th c.) |
| Sons | right **by birth**, in father's lifetime | only **after father's death** |
| Region | most of India | Bengal, Assam |

- **2021:** Mitakshara upper castes / Dayabhaga lower ✗ · sons' rights ✓ · male-only vs both ✗ → **(b) 2 only**.
- **Vineeta Sharma (2020):** daughters are coparceners by birth (after the 2005 amendment).
- **Jurists (1995):** **Vijnaneshvara, Hemadri, Jimutavahana** — not Rajashekhara (poet).
- **Mahattara, Pattakila = village headmen** (2014).
- **Araghatta = water-wheel with pots** (2016; also 2025, H9).
- Society: new jatis, wider untouchability, devadasis, Brahmana grantees, *stridhana*.
- **Literature:** Kannada **Pampa, Ponna, Ranna**; *Kavirajamarga* · Telugu **Nannaya** · Sanskrit **Kalhana**, Bilhana, Somadeva (*Kathasaritsagara*), **Jayadeva** (*Gita Govinda*).
