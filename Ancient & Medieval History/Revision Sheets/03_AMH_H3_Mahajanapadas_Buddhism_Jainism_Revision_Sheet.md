## PART A — STATES AND CITIES

### 2. Second urbanisation (c. 600 BCE)

- Chain: **iron** (clears Ganga forest) → **wet-rice surplus** → crafts, **shrenis** (guilds), traders → **towns** (Rajagriha, Shravasti, Kaushambi, Varanasi, Champa, Ujjayini) → **NBPW** (Northern Black Polished Ware) → **punch-marked coins**, mostly **silver**.
- **2026:** Pali texts give first definite coin terms — **kahapana, nikkha, kamsa, kakanika** — corroborated by punch-marked silver coins → linked to **both** urban life **and** money economy.

### 3. The sixteen mahajanapadas (Anguttara Nikaya)

- **1997 map:** Matsya, Avanti, Vatsa, Anga.

### 4. Gana-sanghas

- Ruled by Kshatriya clan assemblies in a **santhagara**; decisions by debate and vote.
- **Vajji** (8 clans; Lichchhavis of Vaishali) · **Mallas** · **Shakyas** (Kapilavastu; Buddha) · **Koliyas** (Buddha's mother) · **Jnatrikas** (Mahavira) · **Moriyas**.
- Less orthodox; lost to monarchies that could tax and keep standing armies; sangha borrowed their procedures.

### 5. Rise of Magadha

- **Haryanka (c. 544–413 BCE):** **Bimbisara** — marriage alliances (Kosala, Lichchhavis, Madra), annexes **Anga**, sends Jivaka to Avanti, Buddha's patron · **Ajatashatru** — kills father; defeats Kosala and **Vajji** (*mahashilakantaka* catapult, *rathamusala* mace-chariot); **1st Buddhist council** · **Udayin** — founds **Pataliputra** at the Ganga–Son confluence.
- **Shishunaga (c. 413–345):** destroys **Avanti** · **Kalashoka** (Kakavarna) — **2nd council at Vaishali**.
- **Nanda (c. 345–321):** **Mahapadma Nanda** — "ekarat", "uprooter of Kshatriyas", first non-Kshatriya empire, conquers Kalinga · **Dhana Nanda** — ruling when **Alexander** reached the Beas, **326 BCE** (2000) → overthrown by Chandragupta Maurya.
- **Why Magadha won:** iron (Rajgir, Gaya) · fertile soil · rivers (Ganga, Son, Champa) · fort capitals (Rajagriha's five hills; Pataliputra water-fort) · elephants · unorthodox society + ambitious rulers.
- **Contemporaries (2005):** Buddha, Mahavira, Bimbisara, Ajatashatru, Prasenajit, Pradyota, Udayana — **not Milinda** (Menander, 2nd c. BCE).
- Order: Haryanka → Shishunaga → Nanda → Maurya · capitals Rajagriha → (Vaishali) → Pataliputra.

## PART B — THE NEW RELIGIONS

### 6. Why they arose

- Costly sacrifice killed plough-cattle · merchants (Vaishyas) low in varna · Sanskrit rituals for the few · Upanishads had already raised karma and rebirth · **shramanas** (ascetics outside the Vedic order) multiply.
- Appeal: **ahimsa**, indifference to varna, **Prakrit/Pali**, **Kshatriya founders** from republics, **merchant and royal patrons**.

### 7. The two lives

| | Buddha | Mahavira |
|---|---|---|
| Dates | trad. c. 563–483 BCE (scholars c. 480–400) | Jain trad. 599–527 BCE (scholars c. 540–468) |
| Born | **Lumbini** (Nepal), near Kapilavastu | **Kundagrama**, near Vaishali |
| Clan | **Shakya**; father Suddhodana | **Jnatrika**; father Siddhartha |
| Mother | **Mahamaya**, Koliya princess (raised by Prajapati Gautami) | **Trishala**, **sister** of Lichchhavi chief **Chetaka** |
| Family | wife Yashodhara, son Rahula | wife Yashoda, daughter Anojja (Priyadarshana) |
| Renounced | at **29** (mahabhinishkramana) | at **30** |
| Liberation | at **35**, **Bodh Gaya**, pipal, on the **Niranjana**: nirvana | at **42**, sal tree, Rijupalika near Jrimbhikagrama: **kaivalya**, Jina |
| First sermon | **Sarnath** (Deer Park): dhamma-chakka-pavattana | — |
| Death | at **80**, **Kushinagar**: mahaparinirvana | at **72**, **Pavapuri** |
| Royal links | Bimbisara, Ajatashatru, Prasenajit | Lichchhavis; Bimbisara's queen Chellana = Chetaka's daughter |
| Taught | **Kosala and Magadha** (most rains at **Shravasti**, Jetavana); never Avanti or Gandhara (2014, 2015) | Magadha, Videha, Anga |

- **Buddha's symbols:** lotus/bull (birth) · horse (renunciation) · Bodhi tree (enlightenment) · wheel (first sermon) · stupa (death).
- **Sujata's** rice-milk ended six years of austerity → Middle Path.
- **2003 key caveat:** compilation accepts "Buddha's mother a Kosalan princess"; fact = **Koliya**. Statement 1 false (Trishala = Chetaka's sister), 3 true (Parshvanatha of Banaras) → (c) by elimination.

### 8. Buddhist teaching

- **Four Noble Truths:** dukkha · its cause, craving (tanha) · its end (nirodha, nirvana) · the **Eightfold Path** (right view, intention, speech, action, livelihood, effort, mindfulness, concentration).
- **Middle Way** between indulgence and self-torture.
- **Anicca** (impermanence), **dukkha**, **anatta** (no permanent self); **pratitya-samutpada** (dependent origination).
- Accepts karma and rebirth; rejects Vedas, sacrifice, creator god, varna as path.
- **Three Jewels:** Buddha, Dhamma, Sangha. **Tipitaka** (Pali): **Vinaya** (rules), **Sutta** (discourses), **Abhidhamma** (philosophy). Jatakas = previous lives.
- **2020 terms:** parivrajaka = wandering renunciant ✓ · shramana = high-status priest ✗ (ascetic outside Vedic order) · upasaka = lay follower ✓.

### 9. Councils

| Council | Place | King | President | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1st, c. 483 BCE | Rajagriha | Ajatashatru | **Mahakassapa** | Upali → Vinaya; Ananda → Sutta |
| 2nd, c. 383 BCE | Vaishali | Kalashoka | Sabakami | first split: Sthaviras vs Mahasanghikas (over discipline) |
| 3rd, c. 250 BCE | Pataliputra | Ashoka | **Moggaliputta Tissa** | Abhidhamma added; missions abroad |
| 4th, 1st c. CE | Kundalavana, Kashmir | Kanishka | **Vasumitra** (vice Ashvaghosha) | Hinayana/Mahayana; Sanskrit (2001 ✓) |

- **Fa-hien did not attend** Kanishka's council (came c. 400 CE) (2004).

### 10. Sects

- **Sthaviravada** (Elders) → **Theravada** ("Hinayana"; Sri Lanka, SE Asia). **Not Mahayana** (2020 ✗).
- **Sarvastivada:** elements exist in past, present, future in latent form (2017 ✓); Kashmir.
- **Sautrantika, Sammitiya** = **Buddhist**, not Jain (2017 ✗).
- **Mahasanghika** → **Lokottaravada** (offshoot ✓ 2020): Buddha "beyond the world" → deification **fostered Mahayana** (2020 ✓).
- **Mahayana:** bodhisattva ideal, Buddha worship, Sanskrit · **Madhyamaka** (Nagarjuna, shunyata) · **Yogachara** (Asanga, Vasubandhu) · later **Vajrayana** (Palas, Tibet).

### 11. Jainism

- **24 tirthankaras:** **Rishabhadeva** (1st) · **Parshvanatha** (23rd, prince of **Banaras**, four vows) · **Mahavira** (24th).
- **Five vows:** ahimsa, satya, asteya, aparigraha + **brahmacharya** (Mahavira's addition).
- **Triratna:** right faith, right knowledge, right conduct · **jiva** in all things · karma as fine matter · **anekantavada**, **syadvada** · no creator god · **sallekhana** (fast unto death).
- Strict ahimsa kept **farmers away** (ploughing kills insects) → religion of **traders** (2000, A and R both true, R explains A).
- **Famine c. 300 BCE:** **Bhadrabahu** went south with **Chandragupta Maurya** (Shravanabelagola; Chandragiri) → **Digambaras**; **Sthulabhadra** stayed → **Shvetambaras**. 2004 trap: Bhadrabahu's followers ≠ Shvetambaras.
- **Councils:** 1st **Pataliputra** (c. 300 BCE, Sthulabhadra; 12 Angas) · 2nd **Valabhi** (5th c. CE, **Devarddhi Kshamashramana**; canon written in **Ardhamagadhi Prakrit**).
- **Patrons:** **Kharavela** of Kalinga, 1st c. BCE (**Hathigumpha**, Udayagiri; 2004 ✓) · Chandragupta Maurya · Rashtrakuta **Amoghavarsha** · Western Gangas (**Gommateshwara**, Shravanabelagola, Chamundaraya c. 983 CE) · Chalukya **Kumarapala** (adviser **Hemachandra**).
- Early Jains did **not** worship images (2004 option d false).

### 12. Compared (2012)

- **Common:** reject **Vedic authority** ✓ · deny **efficacy of rituals** ✓ · no creator · karma and rebirth · ahimsa (degree differs) · Kshatriya founders · Prakrit/Pali · sangha + lay followers.
- **Buddhist only:** **Middle Path** (avoid extremes) · anatta · Four Truths · nirvana in this life · spread abroad · faded in India c. 1200 CE.
- **Jain only:** extreme austerity · jiva in stones · syadvada · five vows · survived in India.

### 13. Stupa, patrons, decline

- **Stupa (2023):** pre-Buddhist burial mound → "Buddhist in origin" ✗ · relic repository ✓ · votive and commemorative ✓ → **only two**.
- **Patrons of Buddhism:** Bimbisara, Ajatashatru, Ashoka, Kanishka, **Ikshvakus** (Nagarjunakonda — **not hostile**, 2006), Harsha, **Palas** (Dharmapala's **Vikramashila**; Nalanda) (2006 ✓).
- **Decline (2010):** Buddha absorbed as **avatar of Vishnu** ✓ (UPSC's answer) · wealthy sangha · shift to Sanskrit and ritual · Bhakti and Shankaracharya · Turkish raids on Nalanda, Vikramashila c. 1200 CE. **Guptas were not anti-Buddhist**; "invading tribes persecuted Buddhists" ✗.

### 14. Other shramanas

- **Ajivikas:** **Makkhali Gosala**, **niyati** (strict fate); **Barabar caves** donated by Ashoka (Nagarjuni caves by his grandson Dasharatha).
- **Charvaka / Lokayata:** materialism (Ajita Kesakambalin among early teachers).
- Purana Kassapa (denied moral causation) · Sanjaya Belatthiputta (agnostic).
