## 2. Who and where

- **Dates:** Rigvedic (early) **c. 1500–1000 BCE** · Later Vedic **c. 1000–600 BCE**.
- **"Aryan" = a language (speech) group**, not a race (1999). Indo-Aryan, a branch of Indo-European (with Avestan, Greek, Latin).
- **Boghazkoi** (Turkey), c. 1400 BCE: treaty names **Indra, Mitra, Varuna, Nasatyas**.
- Origin theories: Max Müller (Central Asia), Tilak (Arctic), Dayananda (Tibet), indigenous; mainstream = migration of Indo-Aryan speakers in the 2nd millennium BCE.
- **RV vs Harappan (2017):** RV rural, pastoral; knew **horse**, spoked-wheel **chariot**, **coat of mail (varman)**; Harappans did not. Indus people did **not** know iron.

## 3. Texts

- **Shruti** (heard): 4 Vedas, each with **Samhita → Brahmana → Aranyaka → Upanishad** (ritual → meaning → philosophy).
- **Rigveda:** 10 mandalas, **1,028 hymns**; II–VII oldest (family books); **III Gayatri** (Vishvamitra, to Savitri); **IX all Soma**; **X Purusha Sukta** (4 varnas, late) and **Nadi Sukta**. Women composers: **Lopamudra** (1995), Ghosha, Apala, Vishvavara.
- **Samaveda:** melodies, root of Indian music · **Yajurveda:** formulae; Shukla (Vajasaneyi), Krishna · **Atharvaveda:** charms, healing; sabha and samiti = two daughters of Prajapati; **kshetra-patni** (2026).
- **Brahmanas:** Aitareya (RV), **Shatapatha** (YV).
- **Upanishads** (~108; Vedanta): **Brihadaranyaka** (Yajnavalkya with **Gargi**, **Maitreyi**) · **Chandogya** (*tat tvam asi*; three ashramas) · **Katha** (Nachiketa–Yama) · **Mundaka** (*Satyameva Jayate*).
- **Vedangas (6):** Shiksha (phonetics) · Kalpa (ritual; **Sulva Sutras**) · Vyakarana (**Panini**) · Nirukta (**Yaska**) · Chhanda (Pingala) · Jyotisha (Lagadha).
- **Upavedas:** Ayurveda (Rig) · Dhanurveda (Yajur) · Gandharvaveda (Sama) · Shilpa/Arthaveda (Atharva).
- **Smriti** (remembered): Dharmasutras, Smritis (Manu, Yajnavalkya), epics, Puranas.
- Oral preservation (pada, krama, jata); **UNESCO: Vedic chanting**, proclaimed 2003.

## 4. Polity and economy

- **Rigvedic:** *jana* under a *rajan* (**gopa janasya**); assemblies **sabha** (elders), **samiti** (people), **vidatha** (oldest; women took part); **bali** voluntary; no standing army.
- **Battle of Ten Kings** on the **Parushni (Ravi):** Sudas of the Bharatas (priest **Vasishtha**) defeats a coalition backed by **Vishvamitra**.
- **Later Vedic:** *janapada*; hereditary kingship; **Rajasuya, Ashvamedha, Vajapeya**; **ratnins**; bali and bhaga compulsory; vidatha disappears; women out of the sabha; Kuru–Panchala dominant.
- **Rigvedic economy:** pastoral; cattle wealth; **gavishti** = war (search for cows); **yava** (barley); plough known; **ayas** = copper/bronze; no coins, no towns; *nishka* = ornament.
- **2026 wells:** **ashma chakra** (stone pulley) + **ahava** (strapped pails) prove well irrigation; **oxen** used before the RV → draught power. Axes and sickles prove nothing about wells.
- **Later Vedic economy:** agrarian; iron = **shyama / krishna ayas** (c. 1000 BCE); **vrihi** (rice), **godhuma** (wheat); **Painted Grey Ware** (Hastinapur, Ahichhatra, Atranjikhera, Bhagwanpura); still no coins or cities.
- **1998 order:** urban culture → iron plough → punch-marked silver coins → gold coins.

## 5. Society

- **Early:** kula (kulapa) → grama → vish → jana; patriarchal; varna loose ("I am a bard, my father a physician, my mother grinds corn"); arya vs dasa/dasyu; women compose hymns, attend vidatha/sabha, adult marriage, **niyoga**.
- **Later:** four hereditary varnas; **gotra** and **gotra exogamy**; **ashramas** (Chandogya names 3; sannyasa added later); women's status declines; philosophers **Gargi, Maitreyi**; **kshetra-patni** in Atharvaveda.
- **1996:** kula, vamsa, gotra = kinship; **kosa = treasury** (odd one out).

---

## 6. Religion and philosophy

- **Early (2012): worship of nature and yajnas.** **Indra** (~250 hymns, *Purandara*), **Agni** (~200, messenger), **Varuna** (guardian of **rita**), Soma, Surya, Savitri, Ushas, Aditi. No temples, no idols.
- **2011:** **rita** = fundamental cosmic and moral law ✓ · **dharma** = duties to self and others ✓.
- **Creation Hymn RV X.129:** ends in doubt ("perhaps he does not know").
- **Later:** **Prajapati, Vishnu, Rudra** rise; Indra and Agni fade; costly ritual; priests dominate.
- **Upanishads:** **atman = brahman**; **karma**, **samsara**, **moksha**; knowledge over ritual → Buddhism and Jainism (H3); later six darshanas (Nyaya, Vaisheshika, Samkhya, Yoga, Mimamsa, Vedanta).
- **Science:** **Baudhayana Sulva Sutra** = right-angled triangle relation (2008); Vedanga Jyotisha (Lagadha); Panini's Ashtadhyayi.

## 7. What survives today (Mains 2023)

- **Little changed:** samskaras (naming, sacred thread, fire-centred marriage, cremation); havan/yajna; Vedic chanting; **gotra exogamy**; Sanskrit liturgy; reverence for rivers, fire, cow.
- **Transformed:** varna → caste (rejected by **Arts 15, 17**, socially persistent); ashrama → joint-family ideals; karma–dharma–moksha vocabulary across Indian religions; sabha–samiti invoked as deliberative roots.
- **Faded:** animal sacrifice; Rajasuya/Ashvamedha; pastoral economy; women's early freedom (later lost, now restored by law).
- **Judgement line:** Vedic India survives as a living grammar of ritual, family and philosophy, and its hierarchy survives as a problem the Constitution seeks to undo.

### Early vs later trap list

iron, rice, PGW, doab → **Later** · vidatha, women in sabha → **Early** · Rajasuya, Ashvamedha, Vajapeya → **Later** · gotra, four ashramas, rigid varna → **Later** · Prajapati/Vishnu/Rudra dominant → **Later** · temples, idols, bhakti → **neither** · coins, cities, mahajanapadas → **neither** (H3).
