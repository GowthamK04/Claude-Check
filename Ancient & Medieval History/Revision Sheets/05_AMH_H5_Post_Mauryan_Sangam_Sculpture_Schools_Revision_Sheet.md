## 2. Sungas and Kanvas

- **Sungas (c. 185–73 BCE):** **Pushyamitra** (Brahmana general) kills **Brihadratha**; Pataliputra + **Vidisha**; **two Ashvamedhas** (Ayodhya inscription of Dhanadeva); **Patanjali** (Mahabhashya); **Agnimitra** = hero of Kalidasa's **Malavikagnimitram**; Bharhut railings (Sunga era).
- **Heliodorus:** Greek envoy of **Antialcidas** (Taxila) at **Bhagabhadra**'s court; **Garuda pillar at Besnagar** (Vidisha) for **Vasudeva** — a Greek *bhagavata*; worshipped as "Khamba Baba".
- **Kanvas (c. 73–28 BCE):** **Vasudeva Kanva** kills last Sunga **Devabhuti**; ended by the **Satavahanas ("Andhras")** (2003: all three statements true).

## 3. Foreigners who became Indian

- **Order (2006):** **Greeks → Sakas → Parthians → Kushanas** ("Great Scythians Parted Kingdoms").
- **Indo-Greeks:** from **Bactria** (1999); Demetrius; **Menander (Milinda)**, capital **Sakala** (Sialkot); **Milindapanho** with monk **Nagasena**; first coins firmly assigned to named kings, with portraits; among first gold coins; **strategos** = military governorship (2000); seeded Gandhara art.
- **Sakas (Scythians):** northern Kshatrapas (Taxila, Mathura); **Western Kshatrapas** (Nashik, **Ujjain**); **Nahapana** defeated by Gautamiputra Satakarni; **Rudradaman I** (c. 130–150 CE): **Junagadh inscription, 150 CE, first long classical Sanskrit inscription**; repaired **Sudarshana** lake.
- **Parthians (Pahlavas):** **Gondophernes**; **St Thomas** legend; Takht-i-Bahi inscription.
- **Kushanas:** **Yuezhi** branch; **Kujula Kadphises** → **Vima Kadphises** (gold coins, Shiva) → **Kanishka** → Huvishka → Vasudeva.
- **Kanishka:** capitals **Purushapura** (Peshawar) and **Mathura**; titles **Devaputra**, Kaisara; **4th council** (Kundalavana, Kashmir; Vasumitra); patron of **Ashvaghosha** (Buddhacharita), Vasumitra, Parshva, **Charaka**, (Nagarjuna by tradition); **headless statue at Mat** (Mathura); coins: Helios, Mithra, Shiva, Buddha ("BODDO"); Silk Road.
- **Kanishka's date:** traditionally **78 CE** (Saka era); **Rabatak inscription** (1993, Afghanistan, Bactrian) → many now say **c. 127 CE**.
- **Takshashila** lay between the **Indus and Jhelum** (2006).

## 4. Satavahanas

- "Andhras" in Puranas; capital **Pratishthana (Paithan)** on Godavari; founder **Simuka**.
- **Satakarni I:** 2 Ashvamedhas; **Naneghat** inscription of queen **Naganika**.
- **Gautamiputra Satakarni** (c. 106–130 CE): crushed **Nahapana** (restruck coins, **Jogaltembhi** hoard); **Nasik inscription by his mother Gautami Balashri** — destroyer of Sakas, Yavanas, Pahlavas; "**ekabamhana**".
- **Vasishthiputra Pulumavi**; **Yajna Sri Satakarni** (ship coins).
- **Features:** **matronymics**; **earliest tax-free land grants** to Brahmanas and Buddhist monks; **lead, potin**, copper coins; **Prakrit** in Brahmi; patronised Brahmanism and Buddhism.
- **Art & literature:** **Karle** chaitya, **Nasik** caves, **Amaravati**; **Hala**'s **Gathasaptashati** (700 Prakrit verses).

## 5. Sangam Tamilakam

- **Tamilakam:** Venkatam (Tirupati) to Kanyakumari; c. 3rd c. BCE – 3rd c. CE.
- **Three Sangams** at Madurai (Pandya patronage); legend: first at "South Madurai", 4,440 years, sunk; only the third's works survive.

| Kingdom | Emblem | Capital · port | Kings |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Chera** | **bow** | Vanji (Karur?) · **Muziris**, Tondi | **Senguttuvan** (Himalayan stone for **Kannagi** image; **Pattini** cult; Gajabahu of Sri Lanka) · **Udiyanjeral** (fed both armies at Kurukshetra — **Chera**, 2026) · Nedunjeral Adan ("Imayavaramban") |
| **Chola** | **tiger** | **Uraiyur** · **Puhar** (Kaveripattinam) | **Karikala** (Battle of **Venni**; **Kallanai / Grand Anicut** on Kaveri) · **Elara** (ruled Sri Lanka) |
| **Pandya** | **fish** | **Madurai** · **Korkai** (pearls) | **Nedunjeliyan** (Battle of **Talaiyalanganam**); Megasthenes: Pandya land ruled by women |

- **2026:** Senguttuvan–Chera ✓, **Udiyanjeral–Chola ✗**, Nedunjeliyan–Pandya ✓ → answer **2 only**. Clue: Chera names end **-cheral/-jeral**; Pandya **-cheliyan/-jeliyan**.
- **2023:** **Korkai, Poompuhar, Muchiri = ports**.
- Ashoka's neighbours (RE II, XIII); Kharavela broke a Tamil confederacy; **Velir** chieftains.

### Literature

- **Tolkappiyam** — grammar and poetics (Tolkappiyar).
- **Ettuthogai** (8 anthologies) — **Purananuru** (puram), **Akananuru** (akam), Kuruntokai, Natrinai…
- **Pattuppattu** (10 idylls) — **Maduraikkanci** (Madurai markets), **Pattinappalai** (Puhar port).
- **Patinenkilkanakku** (18 minor) — **Tirukkural** (**Tiruvalluvar**, 1,330 couplets: virtue, wealth, love).
- **Twin epics (post-Sangam):** **Silappatikaram** (**Ilango Adigal**; Kannagi, Kovalan; Puhar → Madurai → Vanji) · **Manimekalai** (**Sittalai Sattanar**; Buddhist; daughter of Kovalan and Madhavi).

### Five tinai

| Tinai | Landscape | Love-mood | God |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kurinji | hills | union | Murugan |
| Mullai | forest-pasture | waiting | Mayon (Vishnu) |
| Marutam | farmland | quarrels / infidelity | Indra (Vendan) |
| Neytal | seashore | pining | Varuna |
| Palai | wasteland | separation | Korravai |

- **Akam** = love (inner); **Puram** = war, kings, ethics (outer).
- **Society:** hero-stones (**nadukal**), bards (panar, viraliyar), Vellalar, Pulaiyar, Parayar; women poets (**Avvaiyar**); Brahmanas, Jains, Buddhists present.
- **2013 line:** undated, no sequence, praise and legend → weak political history; landscapes, markets, ports, daily life → vivid social history, confirmed by Arikamedu, Pattanam, Keeladi.

## 6. Indo-Roman trade

- **Monsoon winds** crossing (credited to **Hippalus**).
- **Sources:** *Periplus of the Erythraean Sea* (1st c. CE), Pliny, Ptolemy, Sangam poems; **Muziris papyrus** (Vienna, 2nd c. CE).
- **Exports:** **pepper (yavanapriya)**, pearls, muslin, ivory, gems. **Imports:** gold and silver coins, wine (amphorae), glass, coral.
- **Sites:** **Arikamedu** (near Puducherry; amphorae, Arretine ware; **Wheeler 1945**) · **Muziris = Pattanam** (Kerala) · Puhar · Korkai · Roman coin hoards.
- Bay of Bengal trade also rode the monsoon (2011 adj.).

## 7. Sculpture schools

| | Gandhara | Mathura | Amaravati |
|---|---|---|---|
| Region | NW: Taxila, Peshawar, Hadda | Braj, Yamuna | Krishna valley, Andhra |
| Stone | **grey-blue schist**; later stucco | **spotted red sandstone** | **white limestone** |
| Patrons | Indo-Greeks, Sakas, **Kushanas** | **Kushanas**, merchants | **Satavahanas**, Ikshvakus |
| Religion | **Buddhist only** | Buddhist, **Jain, Brahmanical** | mainly Buddhist |
| Influence | Greek, Roman, Central Asian | **indigenous (yaksha)** | indigenous, narrative |
| Face | Apollo-like, calm, spiritual | round, smiling, energetic | slender, graceful |
| Hair | wavy curls, top-knot | shaven / **snail-shell curls** | small curls |
| Robe | **heavy toga folds, both shoulders** | **thin, right shoulder bare** | light |
| Style | realism; Corinthian, acanthus, Atlas | scalloped halo; abhaya; yakshis | **crowded dynamic reliefs; tribhanga** |
| Famous | Bimaran casket; Fasting Buddha | **Headless Kanishka (Mat)**; Katra Buddha | Amaravati, Nagarjunakonda railings |

- **Gandhara influences:** **Greek** — realism, Apollo face, wavy hair, Corinthian capitals, acanthus, vines, Atlas, Herakles → Vajrapani · **Roman** — toga drapery, portrait realism, narrative panels, stucco · **Central Asian** — tunics, trousers, boots, Kushan portraits, Silk Road spread (Hadda, Bamiyan) · **Indian core** — Buddhist themes, mudras, halo, ushnisha, urna.
- **2014 verdict:** Greeks → face and decorative vocabulary; Romans → drapery, realism, storytelling → "as much to Rome as to Greece" is fair; spirit Indian.
- First Buddha image: Gandhara or Mathura — debated (both c. 1st c. CE).
