## 2. Where & when

- **Names:** "Indus Valley" (Marshall) · **"Harappan"** (type site = first excavated; use this) · "Indus–Sarasvati" (contested).
- **Phases (BCE):** Early **3300–2600** (Kot Diji, Amri, Kalibangan I) · **Mature 2600–1900** (the cities) · Late **1900–1300** (localised).
- **Forerunner:** Mehrgarh, Balochistan, Neolithic, farming c. **7000 BCE** — NOT Harappan.
- Larger area than contemporary Egypt or Mesopotamia; striking **uniformity**.
- **Discovery:** 1826 Masson sees mounds · 1856 Brunton brothers use Harappa bricks for the Lahore–Multan railway · 1875 Cunningham publishes a seal · **1921 Daya Ram Sahni, Harappa** · **1922 R. D. Banerji, Mohenjo-daro** · **1924 John Marshall announces**.

## 3. How they built (Mains core)

- **Planned before built:** platforms and grid laid first, houses fitted in.
- **Seven features:** (1) citadel (raised, walled, west) + lower town (larger, east); Dholavira **three parts** (castle + bailey, middle town, lower town) (2) grid streets, right angles, entrances from lanes (3) bricks **1 : 2 : 4**; baked (M-daro, Harappa), mud-brick (Kalibangan), stone (Dholavira) (4) covered drains linked to houses, inspection holes (5) courtyard houses, paved bathing areas, private wells — **~700 wells** at Mohenjo-daro (6) **Great Bath ~12 × 7 m, 2.4 m deep, steps both ends, bitumen**; granaries; pillared halls; Lothal dockyard; Dholavira open ground (7) **no palaces, temples, royal tombs**.
- **Analytical phrase:** civic, not monumental.
- **Counter-cases:** Chanhudaro no citadel · Banawali no strict grid · Kalibangan mud-brick, lower town walled · "granary" may be a Great Hall · "dockyard" may be a reservoir · "broad uniformity with regional variation".

### Water

- **Dholavira chain:** monsoon runoff → **Mansar (N), Manhar (S)** → **check dams**/bunds → **stone channels** → **16+ rock-cut, stone-lined reservoirs** (cascading) → city use; street & roof runoff drained back.
- **27 July 2021:** Dholavira = **India's 40th World Heritage Site**, **first Harappan site in India** (Mohenjo-daro listed 1980).
- Mohenjo-daro: wells. Lothal: dockyard. Shortughai: canals. No large canal network.

## 4. How they lived

- **Crops:** wheat, barley, lentil, chickpea, pea, sesame, mustard; **cotton** (2011; Mehrgarh seeds earlier; M-daro cloth); **rice husk at Lothal, Rangpur**; millets in Gujarat.
- **Animals:** cattle, sheep, goat, buffalo, pig.
- **Plough:** field at Kalibangan; terracotta models at Banawali & Cholistan.
- **Crafts:** beads (carnelian, jasper, crystal, steatite, faience) at Chanhudaro & Lothal; shell at Nageshwar & Balakot; copper/bronze (tin imported); **spindle whorls, no spinning wheel** (2026).
- **Weights:** chert cubes, unmarked; **binary** small (1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32…), decimal large; ivory scale Lothal, shell scale M-daro.
- **Trade names:** **Meluhha = Indus region · Dilmun = Bahrain · Magan = Oman**. Sargon of Akkad c. 2350 BCE: ships from Meluhha. Harappan seals & etched carnelian beads at Ur; Persian Gulf seal at Lothal. **No coins.**
- **Raw materials:** lapis — Badakhshan via Shortughai · copper — Khetri (Ganeshwar–Jodhpura), Oman · tin — Afghanistan · carnelian — Bharuch · shell — Gujarat coast · gold — south India · steatite — S. Rajasthan, N. Gujarat.
- **2026 inference rule:** whorls → home spinning ✓ (factory ✗) · scales/weights → measurement science ✓ (coins ✗) · big private houses with wells → private property ✓ (common property ✗) · seals abroad → trade ✓ (empire ✗) · no palaces → power not displayed ✓ (no rulers ✗).

## 5. What they believed and wrote

| Object | Material | Site |
|---|---|---|
| Dancing Girl | **bronze**, lost-wax, ~10.5 cm, found 1926, National Museum New Delhi | Mohenjo-daro |
| Priest-King | **steatite**, trefoil shawl, Karachi | Mohenjo-daro |
| Male torso | **red sandstone** | Harappa |
| Mother goddess, toy carts | terracotta | many |
| Bronze hoard (chariot, rhino, elephant, buffalo) | bronze, date debated | Daimabad |

- **Seals:** square, steatite, fired; **unicorn most common**; humped bull, elephant, tiger, rhinoceros, buffalo, gharial; **no cow, no lion, no horse**.
- **Pashupati seal:** horned figure cross-legged, elephant, tiger, rhinoceros, buffalo around, two deer below; Marshall's "proto-Shiva" (interpretation).
- **Pottery:** red with black paint, wheel-made.
- **Religion:** mother goddess; male deity (2013: male + female deities); pipal; animals; **fire altars at Kalibangan & Lothal**; Great Bath ritual (probable). UPSC 2011: **"predominantly secular"**.
- **Burial:** extended, head north; Harappa R-37 & H; Lothal paired burial.
- **Authority:** "faceless" — standards everywhere, no ruler's image.
- **Script:** ~**400 signs**; mainly **right to left**; average ~5 signs; **undeciphered** because short texts, no bilingual, unknown language. Dholavira **signboard, 10 large signs**, near citadel's north gate. **Jan 2025: Tamil Nadu CM's US$1 million prize** for decipherment.

### Absence list

No iron · no domesticated horse evidence (Surkotada disputed) · no horse chariots · no coat of mail/helmet · no palaces, temples, royal tombs · no cow or lion on seals · no coins · no spinning wheel · no large canal irrigation · no deciphered script.

## 6. How it ended and what remains

- **c. 1900 BCE:** cities shrink, script/seals fade, trade fades, villages multiply east (Haryana, W. UP) and south (Gujarat) = **deurbanisation, not disappearance**.
- **Theories graded:** river shift — Ghaggar–Hakra dries (Lambrick on the Indus) **strong** · 4.2 ka aridity c. 2200 BCE — **Mawmluh Cave stalagmite → Meghalayan Age, ICS 2018**; pollen, Gurdip Singh 1971 **strong** · floods/tectonics — M. R. Sahni, Raikes **local** · ecology — Fairservis **partial** · trade collapse **partial** · Aryan invasion — Wheeler ("Indra stands accused", 1947) **rejected** by G. F. Dales (1964).
- **Continuities:** bullock cart · cotton · bangles · mother goddess · pipal · fire rituals · weights · courtyard house · tanks/stepwells.
- **Lessons for cities (2014):** drains first · city-scale water harvesting · building standards · zoning. **Limits:** tens of thousands not millions; no migration/density pressures; governance unknown → **principles, not templates**.

## 7. In the news 2019–2026

- **Sep 2019** — Rakhigarhi genome, *Cell* (Shinde, Reich et al.): no detectable steppe-pastoralist ancestry; largest component related to ancient Iranians; one individual only.
- **27 Jul 2021** — Dholavira: India's 40th WHS, first Harappan site in India.
- **Jan 2025** — US$1 m prize for deciphering the script (Tamil Nadu).
- **2025** — Ratadiya Ri Dheri, Jaisalmer: first Harappan settlement reported in Rajasthan's Thar; rural, Mature Harappan; kilns like Kanmer and Mohenjo-daro.
- **Jan 2026** — ASI begins a fresh Rakhigarhi excavation focused on town planning.
