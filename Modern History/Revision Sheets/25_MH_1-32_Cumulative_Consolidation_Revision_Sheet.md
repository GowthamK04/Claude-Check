## 4. The 250-word answer — skeleton to memorise (2021, Moderates)
1. **Verdict:** built the base; their method had to be replaced.
2. **All-India platform** — Congress 1885.
3. **Economic indictment** — Naoroji, Ranade, R. C. Dutt → Swadeshi, khadi.
4. **Constitutional demands** — councils, Indianisation, civil liberties → 1892, 1909, 1919 → Karachi 1931.
5. **Secular liberal cadre** — Gokhale (Gandhi's political guru); Servants of India Society 1905.
6. **Limits** — narrow urban base; petitions; Aurobindo's "New Lamps for Old" (1893).
7. **Close** — ideas and institution from the Moderates; method and masses from Extremists and Gandhi. Bipan Chandra: ideological foundations — a base, not a building.

## 6. Cross-topic traps (the ones that recur across Seq 1–24)
1. **Viceroys:** VPA — Lytton · Ilbert Bill — Ripon · Partition — Curzon · Rowlatt — Chelmsford · Gandhi–Irwin — Irwin · August Offer and Quit India — Linlithgow.
2. **Outcome → movement:** Tenancy Act 1885 = Pabna · DAR Act 1879 = Deccan Riots · Santhal Parganas = Santhal hul · CNT Act 1908 = Birsa · Champaran Agrarian Act 1918 = Champaran.
3. **Order:** Surat 1907 → Komagata Maru 1914 → Lucknow 1916 → Montagu 1917 → Rowlatt 1919.
4. **Who said it:** "microscopic minority" — Dufferin · "post-dated cheque" — Gandhi on Cripps · "Do or Die" — Gandhi 1942 · "Swaraj is my birthright" — Tilak · "Sedition has become my religion" — Gandhi at Dandi.
5. **Statement traps:** NCM = **surrender** titles · Cripps CA = **elected** · Wood = English **only at higher levels** · 1881 Factories Act = **no** wages, **no** unions · Ryotwari ≠ Cornwallis · Tebhaga = **one-third** · Eka = rent, Bardoli = revenue.
6. **Cabinet Mission members:** Pethick-Lawrence, Cripps, A. V. Alexander — **Wavell was not a member**.

## 7. Repair protocol
- Error types: **F** fact/attribution · **Ch** chronology · **P** precision (statement traps) · **C** cause/purpose.
- Cluster by **type + topic**; ignore isolated slips in strong areas.
- Repair: open **only** that topic's revision sheet; **5 min per cluster**; one corrected line per error.
- P errors → re-read stems, not notes. Ch errors → redraw that Metro line. C errors → rehearse the frames in §1.
- Re-test with changed framing; then **stop**. Write the two weakest Seq numbers in the tracker for a second pass.
